About Sekai

Sekai (世界, "world" in Japanese) shows which countries are on a public holiday, today and on any day this year and next. It is run by KeiK LLC, a small app studio in Japan.

What is on the site

The world map lights up every country that has a nationwide public holiday on the selected day. The country pages list each holiday with its date, local name and a short note on what the day is, and mark holidays that apply only to some regions. The monthly pages show the whole world day by day. Everything is available in 35 languages.

Where the data comes from

Holiday dates come from Nager.Date. The notes and translations are written for our World Holidays app. Country borders on the map are from Natural Earth (public domain) and are a simplified drawing, not a statement on any territorial question. Holiday dates can change by government decision, so please confirm with an official source before you book or sign anything.

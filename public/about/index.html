About Sekai

Sekai (世界, "world" in Japanese) is run by KeiK LLC, a small app studio in Japan. The site has two parts.

World Holidays

Public holidays for 246 countries and regions, in 35 languages, for this year and next. Each page lists the date, the local name, a short note on what the day is, and whether it applies to the whole country or only some regions. The dates come from Nager.Date; the notes and translations are written for our World Holidays app. Holiday dates can change by government decision, so please confirm with an official source before you book or sign anything.

News dashboard

The front page is a live dashboard of headlines and public data for Japan and the world. It is built on World Monitor by Elie Habib, which is free software under the AGPL-3.0 license. Our modified source code is public at github.com/keik24/sekai. Headlines link to the original publishers, who own their articles.

このサイトについて

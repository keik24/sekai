🇸🇽 Sint Maarten Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Sint Maarten. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNieuwjaarsdag

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayGoede Vrijdag

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayEerste paasdag

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayTweede paasdag

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Mon, Apr 27
    King's DayKoningsdag

    King's Day is a public holiday across the Kingdom of the Netherlands on 27 April, or 26 April when the 27th is a Sunday. It celebrates King Willem-Alexander's birthday with orange-themed festivities and street markets.

  • Thu, Apr 30
    Carnival DayCarnavalsdag

    Carnival Day is a public holiday during each country's carnival season, whose timing varies: in Angola it falls before Lent, in Saint Kitts and Nevis around New Year, and in Sint Maarten in late April.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayDag van de Arbeid

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayHemelvaartsdag

    Ascension Day commemorates the Christian belief that Jesus ascended into heaven. It falls on the 40th day of Easter (39 days after Easter Sunday), always a Thursday, and is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, May 24
    PentecostEerste Pinksterdag

    Whit Sunday, also called Pentecost, is the Christian feast celebrating the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles fifty days after Easter. It is observed on the seventh Sunday after Easter Sunday.

  • Wed, Jul 1
    Emancipation DayDag van de Bevrijding

    Emancipation Day commemorates the abolition of slavery. It is observed on different dates in many Caribbean countries and territories and elsewhere in the Americas, each marking its own emancipation history.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Constitution DayDag van de Constitutie

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Sint Maarten DaySint-Maartensdag

    Sint Maarten Day is a public holiday on 11 November, the feast of Saint Martin of Tours, on which Columbus is said to have sighted and named the island in 1493. It is shared with French Saint-Martin.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayEerste Kerstdag

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of ChristmasTweede Kerstdag

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

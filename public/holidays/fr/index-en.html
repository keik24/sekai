🇫🇷 France Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in France. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

11 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayJour de l'an

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Thu, Mar 5
    Arrival of the GospelArrivée de l'ÉvangileRegional

    Arrivée de l'Évangile commemorates the arrival of Protestant missionaries in French Polynesia on 5 March 1797. It is a public holiday in the territory and marks the founding of the Christian church in the islands.

  • Thu, Mar 12
    Mi-CarêmeRegional

    Mi-Carême, or Mid-Lent, is the Thursday in the middle of Lent, about three weeks after Ash Wednesday. In places such as France and Guadeloupe it is traditionally marked by masquerades and carnival parades.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayVendredi saintRegional

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayLundi de Pâques

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Mon, Apr 27
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Tue, Apr 28
    Saint Pierre Chanel DaySaint Pierre ChanelRegional

    Saint Pierre Chanel Day is a public holiday in Wallis and Futuna observed on 28 April. It honours Saint Peter Chanel (1803–1841), the French Marist missionary who was martyred on Futuna and is the patron saint of Oceania.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayFête du Travail

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Fri, May 8
    Victory DayFête de la Victoire

    Victory Day commemorates the end of a war or a decisive military victory. The specific date and conflict marked differ by country, with notable examples including the end of World War II in Europe celebrated on 8 or 9 May.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayAscension

    Ascension Day commemorates the Christian belief that Jesus ascended into heaven. It falls on the 40th day of Easter (39 days after Easter Sunday), always a Thursday, and is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Fri, May 22
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Mon, May 25
    Whit MondayLundi de Pentecôte

    Whit Monday, also called Pentecost Monday, is the Christian holiday on the day after Pentecost, the seventh Sunday after Easter. It is a public holiday in many countries in Europe, Africa and the Caribbean.

  • Wed, May 27
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Thu, May 28
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Wed, Jun 10
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saints Peter and Paul's DaySaints Pierre et PaulRegional

    Saints Pierre et Paul is a public holiday in Wallis and Futuna on 29 June, honouring the apostles Peter and Paul.

  • Tue, Jul 14
    National DayFête nationale

    National Day is the designated date on which a country celebrates its nationhood, often marking independence, unification, the founding of a republic, or the birthday of a sovereign. The date varies by country.

  • Tue, Jul 21
    Victor Schoelcher DayFête de Victor SchoelcherRegional

    Fête de Victor Schoelcher is observed on 21 July in French overseas territories to honour Victor Schoelcher, the French abolitionist who drafted the 1848 decree ending slavery in France's colonies.

  • Wed, Jul 29
    Territory DayFête du TerritoireRegional

    Territory Day is a public holiday in Christmas Island on the first Monday of October, marking its transfer to Australia in 1958, and in Wallis and Futuna on 29 July, marking its 1961 status as a French overseas territory.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssomption

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Thu, Sep 24
    Citizenship DayFête de la CitoyennetéRegional

    Citizenship Day falls on different dates in some territories: 4 November in the Northern Mariana Islands, where residents became US citizens in 1986, and 24 September in New Caledonia, marking the French annexation of 1853.

  • Fri, Oct 9
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayToussaint

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Armistice DayArmistice

    Armistice Day, observed on 11 November, commemorates the 1918 armistice that ended hostilities of the First World War. It is a public holiday in many former Allied countries and is also known as Remembrance Day.

  • Fri, Nov 20
    Matāri'iRegional

    Matāri'i is a Tahitian observance marking the rise of the Pleiades star cluster around November, signalling the start of the season of abundance (Matāri'i i ni'a) in French Polynesia.

  • Sun, Dec 20
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavageRegional

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNoël

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DaySaint ÉtienneRegional

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

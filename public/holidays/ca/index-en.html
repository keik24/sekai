🇨🇦 Canada Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Canada. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

5 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Family DayRegional

    Family Day is a public holiday observed in several countries to celebrate the importance of family. The specific date varies, with Canadian provinces and South Africa among the better-known observers.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Heritage DayRegional

    Heritage Day celebrates cultural heritage. South Africa observes it on 24 September to honour the country's diverse cultures, while in Canada it is a holiday in some provinces in February.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Islander DayRegional

    A statutory holiday in the Canadian province of Prince Edward Island on the third Monday of February, giving islanders a winter holiday.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Louis Riel DayRegional

    A provincial holiday in Manitoba, Canada, on the third Monday of February, honouring Métis leader Louis Riel, founder of the province.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, May 18
    National Patriots' DayRegional

    A statutory holiday in Quebec, Canada, on the Monday before May 25, honoring the Patriotes of the 1837–1838 rebellions; it falls on the same day as Victoria Day elsewhere.

  • Mon, May 18
    Victoria DayRegional

    Victoria Day is a Canadian public holiday observed on the Monday before 25 May. It honours Queen Victoria (1819–1901), whose reign saw Canadian Confederation in 1867, and unofficially marks the start of the Canadian summer season.

  • Sun, Jun 21
    National Indigenous Peoples DayNational Aboriginal DayRegional

    A statutory holiday in Canada's Northwest Territories and Yukon on 21 June, the summer solstice, honouring First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples; it was renamed National Indigenous Peoples Day in 2017.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Saint-Jean-Baptiste Day (Quebec National Holiday)Saint Jean Baptiste DayRegional

    Quebec's National Holiday on 24 June, the feast of Saint John the Baptist, the patron saint of French Canadians.

  • Wed, Jul 1
    Canada Day

    Canada Day, observed on 1 July, is the national day of Canada. It commemorates the union of the British North American colonies into the Dominion of Canada under the British North America Act on 1 July 1867.

  • Wed, Jul 1
    Memorial DayRegional

    Memorial Day is a day of remembrance for those who died in service to their country. In the United States it falls on the last Monday of May; other countries hold similar days on other dates, some also honouring disaster victims.

  • Thu, Jul 9
    Nunavut DayRegional

    A territorial holiday in Nunavut, Canada, on 9 July, marking the 1993 passage of the Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act and the Nunavut Act.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    British Columbia DayRegional

    A statutory holiday in the Canadian province of British Columbia, held on the first Monday of August as a summer long weekend.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    Civic HolidayRegional

    A public holiday in the Northwest Territories and Nunavut, Canada, on the first Monday of August.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    New Brunswick DayRegional

    A civic holiday in the Canadian province of New Brunswick on the first Monday of August, celebrating the province's heritage and culture.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    Saskatchewan DayRegional

    A statutory holiday in the Canadian province of Saskatchewan on the first Monday of August, celebrating the province's history and people.

  • Mon, Aug 17
    Discovery DayRegional

    Discovery Day is observed in the Cayman Islands on the third Monday of May, recalling the islands' sighting by Columbus in 1503, and in Canada's Yukon on the third Monday of August, marking the 1896 Klondike gold discovery.

  • Mon, Sep 7
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Wed, Sep 30
    National Day for Truth and ReconciliationRegional

    The National Day for Truth and Reconciliation is a Canadian federal holiday on 30 September. It honours the children who never returned home and the survivors of the residential school system.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Thanksgiving DayRegional

    Thanksgiving Day is a holiday for giving thanks for the harvest and the past year's blessings. Each place sets its own date, for example the fourth Thursday of November in US territories and the second Monday of October in Canada.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Remembrance DayRegional

    Remembrance Day honours those who died in war. Bermuda, Canada and the Cayman Islands mark it around 11 November, the 1918 Armistice, while Croatia observes it on 18 November for the victims of the Homeland War and Vukovar.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing DayRegional

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

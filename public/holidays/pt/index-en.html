🇵🇹 Portugal Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Portugal. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAno Novo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Thu, Mar 19
    Saint Joseph's DayDia de São JoséRegional

    Saint Joseph's Day is the Christian feast honouring Saint Joseph, the husband of Mary and earthly father of Jesus. It is observed on 19 March by Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some other Christian traditions.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridaySexta-feira Santa

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPáscoa

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Tue, Apr 21
    Our Lady of MércolesDia de Nossa Senhora de MércolesRegional

    A municipal holiday in Castelo Branco, Portugal, honouring its patroness Our Lady of Mércoles. The pilgrimage is held on the second Sunday after Easter, and the following Tuesday is the holiday.

  • Sat, Apr 25
    Freedom DayDia da Liberdade

    Freedom Day celebrates freedom from foreign rule or oppression. Malta marks it on 31 March, Portugal on 25 April for the 1974 Carnation Revolution, and South Africa on 27 April for its first democratic elections in 1994.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayDia do Trabalhador

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Tue, May 12
    Saint Joanna PrincessDia de Santa JoanaRegional

    The municipal holiday of Aveiro, Portugal, on 12 May, honouring Saint Joanna, the Portuguese princess who lived in Aveiro's convent and died there on 12 May 1490.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayQuinta-feira da AscensãoRegional

    Ascension Day commemorates the Christian belief that Jesus ascended into heaven. It falls on the 40th day of Easter (39 days after Easter Sunday), always a Thursday, and is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Fri, May 22
    Leiria Municipal HolidayDia do Município de LeiriaRegional

    A municipal holiday in Leiria, Portugal, on 22 May, celebrating the creation of the Diocese of Leiria in 1545.

  • Sat, May 23
    Portalegre Municipal DayDia do Município de PortalegreRegional

    A municipal holiday in Portalegre, Portugal, on 23 May, the city's official municipal day.

  • Mon, May 25
    Azores DayDia da Região Autónoma dos AçoresRegional

    A public holiday in the Portuguese autonomous region of the Azores, held on Pentecost Monday, which coincides with the islands' Holy Spirit festivities.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiCorpo de Deus

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Wed, Jun 10
    Day of Portugal, Camões and the Portuguese CommunitiesDia de Portugal, de Camões e das Comunidades Portuguesas

    Portuguese national day on 10 June, the death date of poet Luís de Camões in 1580, celebrating Portugal and Portuguese communities worldwide.

  • Sat, Jun 13
    Saint Anthony's DayDia de Santo AntónioRegional

    Saint Anthony's Day is a local holiday in parts of Andorra, Spain and Portugal. Depending on the place it honours Anthony the Abbot on 17 January or Anthony of Padua on 13 June, notably in Lisbon.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Saint John's DayDia de São JoãoRegional

    The feast of Saint John the Baptist, usually on 24 June, a local holiday in cities such as Porto and Braga in Portugal, Florence, Genoa and Turin in Italy, and in Alagoas, Brazil.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saint Peter's DayDia de São PedroRegional

    The feast of Saint Peter on 29 June, observed as a local holiday in parts of Andorra and Portugal and in the Brazilian state of Alagoas.

  • Wed, Jul 1
    Madeira DayDia da Região Autónoma da Madeira e das Comunidades MadeirensesRegional

    The regional holiday of the Autonomous Region of Madeira in Portugal, held on 1 July. It celebrates the region's autonomy and the Madeiran communities abroad.

  • Sat, Jul 4
    Feast of Queen Saint IsabelDia de Santa IsabelRegional

    A municipal holiday in Coimbra, Portugal, on 4 July, honouring the city's patroness, Queen Saint Elizabeth of Portugal (Rainha Santa Isabel), who died on that day in 1336.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssunção de Nossa Senhora

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Thu, Aug 20
    Feast of Our Lady of Agony (Viana do Castelo)Dia de Nossa Senhora da AgoniaRegional

    A municipal holiday in Viana do Castelo, Portugal, on 20 August, the feast of Nossa Senhora da Agonia, centre of one of the country's largest traditional festivals.

  • Sat, Aug 22
    Our Lady of GracesDia de Nossa Senhora das GraçasRegional

    The municipal holiday of Bragança, Portugal, on 22 August, the feast of Our Lady of Graces, the city's patroness, closed by a large procession from the cathedral.

  • Mon, Sep 7
    Faro Municipal DayDia do Município de FaroRegional

    A municipal holiday in Faro, Portugal, on 7 September, marking the day in 1540 when King John III raised Faro from town to city.

  • Tue, Sep 15
    Bocage Day (Setúbal)Dia de BocageRegional

    A municipal holiday in Setúbal, Portugal, on 15 September, the birthday of the poet Manuel Maria Barbosa du Bocage, who was born in the city in 1765.

  • Mon, Sep 21
    Saint Matthew's DayDia de São MateusRegional

    The feast of Saint Matthew on 21 September, a local holiday in Salerno, Italy, and in Viseu, Portugal, where both towns honour him as patron.

  • Mon, Oct 5
    Republic DayImplantação da República

    Republic Day is a national holiday observed by many countries to commemorate the founding of their republic or the adoption of a republican form of government. The specific date varies by country.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayDia de Todos os Santos

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Fri, Nov 27
    Guarda Municipal HolidayDia do Município da GuardaRegional

    The municipal holiday of Guarda, Portugal, on 27 November, marking the charter (foral) granted by King Sancho I in 1199 that founded the town as a city.

  • Tue, Dec 1
    Restoration of Independence DayRestauração da Independência

    Restoration of Independence Day is a national holiday in Latvia (4 May, marking 1990), Portugal (1 December, marking 1640) and Timor-Leste (20 May, marking the restoration of independence in 2002).

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionImaculada Conceição

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDia de Natal

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    First Octave (Madeira)Primeira OitavaRegional

    A regional public holiday in the Portuguese autonomous region of Madeira on December 26, extending the Christmas celebrations into the day after.

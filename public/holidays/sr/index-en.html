🇸🇷 Suriname Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Suriname. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

15 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNieuwjaarsdag

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Chinese New YearChinees Nieuwjaar

    Chinese New Year, also called the Spring Festival, marks the start of the lunisolar Chinese calendar. It falls between 21 January and 20 February and is the most important traditional holiday in China and across the Chinese diaspora.

  • Tue, Mar 3
    HoliHoli-Phagwa

    Holi is the Hindu spring festival of colours, celebrating the triumph of good over evil. It falls after the full moon of Phalguna, usually in March, and is a public holiday in India and Suriname, where it is also called Phagwa.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Ied-Ul-Fitre (geschat)
  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayGoede Vrijdag

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayTweede Paasdag

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayDag van de Arbeid

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Wed, May 27
    Ied-Ul-Adha (geschat)
  • Wed, Jul 1
    Day of Freedoms (Keti Koti)Dag der Vrijheden

    The Day of Freedoms, also called Keti Koti, is a public holiday in Suriname on 1 July. It commemorates the abolition of slavery in 1863 and is celebrated with music, traditional dress and festivities.

  • Sun, Aug 9
    Indigenous Peoples' DayDag der Inheemsen

    Indigenous People Day is a public holiday in Suriname on 9 August, honouring the country's indigenous peoples. The date coincides with the UN International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples.

  • Sat, Oct 10
    Day of the MaroonsDag der Marrons

    The Day of the Maroons is a public holiday in Suriname on 10 October. It honours the Maroons, descendants of Africans who escaped slavery and built free communities in the interior, and recalls the peace treaty of 1760.

  • Sun, Nov 8
    DiwaliDivali

    Diwali, also called Deepavali, is the Hindu festival of lights celebrated over five days in autumn. It symbolises the victory of light over darkness and is a major holiday in India, Nepal, and across the Hindu diaspora.

  • Wed, Nov 25
    Independence DayOnafhankelijkheidsdag

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayEerste Kerstdag

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of ChristmasTweede Kerstdag

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

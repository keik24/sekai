🇹🇩 Chad Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Chad. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    International Women's Day

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Mon, Mar 9
    International Women's Day

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-Fitr

    Eid al-Fitr, the Festival of Breaking the Fast, marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It falls on the first day of Shawwal and is one of the two major Islamic holidays.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-Adha

    Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two major Islamic holidays. It honours Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and coincides with the Hajj pilgrimage on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah.

  • Tue, Aug 11
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Tue, Aug 25
    Prophet Muhammad's BirthdayMawlid

    Mawlid, the Prophet's Birthday, commemorates the birth of Muhammad, the founder of Islam. It is observed by many Muslims on the 12th day of Rabi al-Awwal in the Islamic calendar, though Shia traditions mark it on the 17th.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' Day

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Sat, Nov 28
    Republic Day

    Republic Day is a national holiday observed by many countries to commemorate the founding of their republic or the adoption of a republican form of government. The specific date varies by country.

  • Tue, Dec 1
    Freedom and Democracy Day

    Freedom and Democracy Day is a Chadian national holiday on 1 December. It commemorates the 1990 coup that brought Idriss Déby to power, ending Hissène Habré's regime, and is officially framed as marking Chad's path to multi-party democracy.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

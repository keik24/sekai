🇰🇷 South Korea Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in South Korea. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

21 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day신정연휴

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Seollal Eve설날 전날

    Seollal Eve is the last day of the lunar year, the day before Korean Lunar New Year, and part of South Korea's three-day Seollal holiday.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Seollal설날

    Korean New Year, called Seollal, is one of the most important traditional holidays in Korea. It falls on the first day of the lunisolar Korean calendar and is observed over three days with family gatherings and ancestral rites.

  • Wed, Feb 18
    Day after Seollal설날 다음날

    The day after Seollal (Korean Lunar New Year) is the second day of the first lunar month and part of South Korea's three-day Seollal holiday.

  • Sun, Mar 1
    Independence Movement Day삼일절

    Independence Movement Day (Samiljeol) is a South Korean national holiday on 1 March. It commemorates the March 1st Movement of 1919, when Koreans declared independence from Japanese colonial rule.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    March First Movement Day (substitute holiday)삼일절 대체 휴일

    A day off given in South Korea when March First Movement Day (1 March) falls on a weekend. The day commemorates the 1919 nationwide protests against Japanese colonial rule.

  • Tue, May 5
    Children's Day어린이날

    Children's Day is an observance celebrating children and promoting their welfare. The United Nations marks it on 20 November, but many countries hold their own Children's Day on varying dates.

  • Sun, May 24
    Buddha's Birthday부처님오신날

    Buddha's Birthday is a public holiday in South Korea and Macau on the eighth day of the fourth lunar month. It celebrates the birth of the Buddha with temple visits and lantern decorations.

  • Mon, May 25
    Buddha's Birthday (substitute holiday)부처님오신날 대체 휴일

    In South Korea, this is the substitute holiday for Buddha's Birthday on the eighth day of the fourth lunar month. It is given on the next working day when the holiday falls on a Saturday, Sunday or another public holiday.

  • Wed, Jun 3
    Local Election Day지방선거일

    Local Election Day is the day on which local or municipal elections are held. In some countries it is a public holiday to facilitate voter participation; the date varies by jurisdiction.

  • Sat, Jun 6
    Memorial Day현충일

    Memorial Day is a day of remembrance for those who died in service to their country. In the United States it falls on the last Monday of May; other countries hold similar days on other dates, some also honouring disaster victims.

  • Fri, Jul 17
    Constitution Day제헌절

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Liberation Day광복절

    Liberation Day is a national observance commemorating the day a country was freed from occupation, dictatorship, or foreign rule. The specific date and historical context vary by country.

  • Mon, Aug 17
    Liberation Day (substitute holiday)광복절 대체 휴일

    In South Korea, this is the substitute holiday for Liberation Day (Gwangbokjeol) on 15 August, which marks the end of Japanese colonial rule in 1945. It is given when 15 August falls on a Saturday, Sunday or another holiday.

  • Thu, Sep 24
    Chuseok Eve추석 전날

    Chuseok Eve falls on the 14th day of the eighth lunar month, the day before the Korean harvest festival, and is part of South Korea's three-day Chuseok holiday.

  • Fri, Sep 25
    Chuseok추석

    Chuseok is a major Korean harvest festival, observed on the 15th day of the 8th lunar month. Families gather to share food, perform ancestral rites, and give thanks for the autumn harvest.

  • Sat, Sep 26
    Day after Chuseok추석 다음날

    The day after Chuseok falls on the 16th day of the eighth lunar month and is part of South Korea's three-day Chuseok harvest festival holiday.

  • Sat, Oct 3
    National Foundation Day개천절

    National Foundation Day, known as Gaecheonjeol, is a South Korean public holiday observed on 3 October. It commemorates the legendary founding of the first Korean kingdom of Gojoseon by Dangun in 2333 BCE.

  • Mon, Oct 5
    National Foundation Day (substitute holiday)개천절 대체 휴일

    In South Korea, this is the substitute holiday for National Foundation Day (Gaecheonjeol) on 3 October. When 3 October falls on a Saturday, Sunday or another public holiday, the next working day becomes a holiday.

  • Fri, Oct 9
    Hangul Day한글날

    Hangul Day is a South Korean public holiday on 9 October. It celebrates the proclamation of Hangul, the Korean alphabet, by King Sejong in the 15th century.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day기독탄신일

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

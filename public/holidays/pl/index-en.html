🇵🇱 Poland Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Poland. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNowy Rok

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyŚwięto Trzech Króli

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayNiedziela Wielkanocna

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayPoniedziałek Wielkanocny

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    National DayŚwięto Państwowe

    National Day is the designated date on which a country celebrates its nationhood, often marking independence, unification, the founding of a republic, or the birthday of a sovereign. The date varies by country.

  • Sun, May 3
    National Day of the Third of MayŚwięto Narodowe Trzeciego Maja

    National Day of the Third of May is a Polish public holiday observed on 3 May. It commemorates the 1791 adoption of the Constitution of 3 May, the first codified national constitution in Europe and the second in the world.

  • Sun, May 24
    PentecostZielone Świątki

    Pentecost is the Christian feast of the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles, fifty days after Easter. It is a public holiday in Poland and Romania, where Romania follows the Orthodox Easter date.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiDzień Bożego Ciała

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryWniebowzięcie Najświętszej Marii Panny

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayUroczystość Wszystkich Świętych

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    National Independence DayNarodowe Święto Niepodległości

    National Independence Day is a national observance held in many countries to commemorate the date they gained independence from another nation or colonial power. The specific date varies by country.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveWigilia Bożego Narodzenia

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayBoże Narodzenie (pierwszy dzień)

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of ChristmasBoże Narodzenie (drugi dzień)

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

🇪🇸 Spain Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Spain. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

8 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAño Nuevo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyEpifanía del Señor

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Sat, Feb 28
    Andalusia DayDía de AndalucíaRegional

    The regional day of Andalusia in Spain, held on 28 February. It commemorates the 1980 referendum in which Andalusians voted for full autonomy for the region.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    Balearic Islands Day (observed Monday)Lunes siguiente a Día de las Islas BalearesRegional

    A replacement day off in the Balearic Islands, Spain, on Monday 2 March 2026, because Balearic Islands Day (1 March) falls on a Sunday.

  • Thu, Mar 19
    Saint Joseph's DaySan JoséRegional

    Saint Joseph's Day is the Christian feast honouring Saint Joseph, the husband of Mary and earthly father of Jesus. It is observed on 19 March by Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some other Christian traditions.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-FitrFiesta del Eid FitrRegional

    Eid al-Fitr, the Festival of Breaking the Fast, marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It falls on the first day of Shawwal and is one of the two major Islamic holidays.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Maundy ThursdayJueves SantoRegional

    Maundy Thursday, also called Holy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayViernes Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayLunes de PascuaRegional

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Thu, Apr 23
    Castile and León DayFiesta de Castilla y LeónRegional

    The regional day of Castile and León, Spain, on April 23, recalling the 1521 Battle of Villalar, where the Comuneros rebels were defeated.

  • Thu, Apr 23
    Saint George's DayDía de San JorgeRegional

    Saint George's Day honours the Christian martyr Saint George. It is a public holiday in Georgia on 23 November; on 23 April it is a holiday in Rio de Janeiro state, in Aragon in Spain, and in some Italian towns that have him as patron.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayFiesta del Trabajo

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Sat, May 2
    Community of Madrid DayFiesta de la Comunidad de MadridRegional

    The regional holiday of the Community of Madrid, Spain, on May 2, recalling the 1808 Dos de Mayo uprising of Madrid's people against French troops.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-AdhaFiesta del Sacrificio-Aid Al AdhaRegional

    Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two major Islamic holidays. It honours Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and coincides with the Hajj pilgrimage on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah.

  • Sat, May 30
    Canary Islands DayDía de CanariasRegional

    A regional holiday in Spain's Canary Islands on 30 May, marking the first session of the Canarian Parliament in 1983.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiRegional

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Tue, Jun 9
    La Rioja DayDía de La RiojaRegional

    A regional holiday in the Spanish autonomous community of La Rioja on 9 June, marking the approval of its Statute of Autonomy in 1982.

  • Tue, Jun 9
    Region of Murcia DayDía de la Región de MurciaRegional

    The regional day of Murcia, Spain, on June 9, marking the approval of the region's Statute of Autonomy in 1982.

  • Sat, Jun 13
    Saint Anthony's DaySan AntonioRegional

    Saint Anthony's Day is a local holiday in parts of Andorra, Spain and Portugal. Depending on the place it honours Anthony the Abbot on 17 January or Anthony of Padua on 13 June, notably in Lisbon.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Saint John the Baptist's DaySan JuanRegional

    This feast honours Saint John the Baptist. In parts of Spain it is a holiday on 24 June, marking his birth, while Romania observes the Synaxis of Saint John the Baptist on 7 January.

  • Sat, Jul 25
    Galician National DayDía Nacional de GaliciaRegional

    The national day of Galicia, Spain, held on 25 July, the feast of Saint James, the patron saint of Galicia and Spain, whose shrine is in Santiago de Compostela.

  • Sat, Jul 25
    Saint James' DaySantiago ApóstolRegional

    The feast of Saint James the Apostle on 25 July, a regional holiday in parts of Spain, in the Argentine province of Mendoza and in places in Brazil.

  • Tue, Jul 28
    Day of the Institutions of CantabriaDía de las Instituciones de CantabriaRegional

    A public holiday in the Spanish region of Cantabria on 28 July, recalling the 1778 assembly at Puente San Miguel that approved the ordinances creating the Province of Cantabria.

  • Wed, Aug 5
    Our Lady of AfricaNuestra Señora de ÁfricaRegional

    A local holiday in Ceuta, Spain, on August 5, the feast of Our Lady of Africa, the city's patroness.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAsunción de la Virgen

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Wed, Sep 2
    Ceuta DayDía de CeutaRegional

    The day of the Spanish autonomous city of Ceuta on September 2, recalling the appointment of Pedro de Meneses as the city's first governor in 1415.

  • Tue, Sep 8
    Asturias DayDía de AsturiasRegional

    The official day of the Principality of Asturias, Spain, held on September 8, the feast of Our Lady of Covadonga, the region's patroness.

  • Tue, Sep 8
    Day of Our Lady of VictoryDía de Nuestra Señora la Virgen de la VictoriaRegional

    A local holiday in the Spanish autonomous city of Melilla on 8 September, the feast of its patroness, the Virgin of Victory. Her image is carried in procession during the city's fair.

  • Tue, Sep 8
    Extremadura DayDía de ExtremaduraRegional

    A public holiday in the Spanish region of Extremadura on 8 September, the feast of Our Lady of Guadalupe, the region's patroness.

  • Fri, Sep 11
    National Day of Catalonia (La Diada)Fiesta Nacional de CataluñaRegional

    La Diada, Catalonia's official holiday on 11 September, recalling the fall of Barcelona to Bourbon troops in 1714 during the War of the Spanish Succession.

  • Tue, Sep 15
    Our Lady of Bien AparecidaLa Bien AparecidaRegional

    A regional holiday in Cantabria, Spain, on September 15, the feast of the Virgen de la Bien Aparecida, patroness of Cantabria.

  • Thu, Sep 17
    Melilla DayDía de MelillaRegional

    A public holiday in the Spanish autonomous city of Melilla on 17 September, marking its capture by Castilian forces under Pedro de Estopiñán in 1497.

  • Fri, Oct 9
    Valencian Community DayDía de la Comunidad ValencianaRegional

    The regional holiday of the Valencian Community, Spain, on 9 October, marking King James I of Aragon's entry into the city of Valencia in 1238.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    National DayFiesta Nacional de España

    National Day is the designated date on which a country celebrates its nationhood, often marking independence, unification, the founding of a republic, or the birthday of a sovereign. The date varies by country.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Saints' Day (substitute holiday)Lunes siguiente a Todos los SantosRegional

    A substitute holiday in several Spanish regions, including Andalusia, Aragon, Madrid and the Canary Islands, on the Monday after All Saints' Day (1 November) when it falls on a Sunday, as in 2026.

  • Thu, Dec 3
    Saint Francis Xavier's DaySan Francisco JavierRegional

    A public holiday in the Spanish region of Navarre on 3 December, the feast of Saint Francis Xavier, the Navarrese-born Jesuit missionary and patron saint of Navarre.

  • Mon, Dec 7
    Constitution Day (Monday holiday)Lunes siguiente a Día de la Constitución EspañolaRegional

    A Monday holiday in several Spanish regions when Constitution Day, 6 December, falls on a Sunday, as in 2026. It commemorates the 1978 referendum approving the Spanish Constitution.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionInmaculada Concepción

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNatividad del Señor

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DaySan EstebanRegional

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Moldova. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

14 public holidays

    New Year's DayAnul Nou

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Wed, Jan 7
    Christmas (Old Style)Nașterea lui Iisus Hristos (Crăciunul pe stil vechi)

    Christmas by the old style is a public holiday in Moldova on 7 and 8 January, following the Julian calendar of the Orthodox Church. It celebrates the birth of Jesus with church services and family meals.

    Christmas (Old Style)Nașterea lui Iisus Hristos (Crăciunul pe stil vechi)

  • Mon, Apr 20
    Day of RejoicingPaștele blajinilor

    The Day of Rejoicing (Paștele Blajinilor) is a public holiday in Moldova on the Monday eight days after Orthodox Easter. Families visit cemeteries to remember their departed relatives and share food at the graves.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' Solidarity DayZiua internaţională a solidarităţii oamenilor muncii

    International Workers' Solidarity Day is a public holiday in Moldova on 1 May, celebrating workers and the labour movement as part of worldwide May Day observances.

  • Sat, May 9
    Europe DayZiua Europei

    Europe Day is observed on 9 May in the European Union to commemorate the 1950 Schuman Declaration that proposed the European Coal and Steel Community, a precursor to the EU. The Council of Europe marks Europe Day on 5 May.

  • Sat, May 9
    Victory Day and Commemoration of Fallen HeroesZiua Victoriei și a comemorării eroilor căzuţi pentru Independenţa Patriei

    Victory Day and Commemoration of the Heroes Fallen for the Independence of the Fatherland is a public holiday in Moldova on 9 May. It marks the end of the Second World War in Europe and honours those who died for the country.

  • Mon, Jun 1
    International Children's DayZiua Ocrotirii Copilului

    International Children's Day, observed on 1 June in many countries, is a celebration honouring children and promoting their welfare. It originated in the 1925 World Conference for the Wellbeing of Children in Geneva.

  • Thu, Aug 27
    Republic of Moldova Independence DayZiua independenţei Republicii Moldova

    Republic of Moldova Independence Day is Moldova's national holiday on 27 August. It marks the 1991 declaration of independence from the Soviet Union.

  • Mon, Aug 31
    National Language DayLimba noastră

    National Language Day (Limba noastră, Our Language) is a public holiday in Moldova on 31 August. It commemorates the 1989 language law that made Romanian the state language and restored the Latin alphabet.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas (New Style)Nașterea lui Iisus Hristos (Crăciunul pe stil nou)

    Christmas by the new style is a public holiday in Moldova on 25 December, following the Gregorian calendar. Moldova also keeps Christmas by the old style on 7 January.

🇱🇰 Sri Lanka Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Sri Lanka. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

26 public holidays
  • Sat, Jan 3
    Duruthu Full Moon Poya Dayදුරුතු පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Duruthu Poya, a Buddhist holiday in Sri Lanka on the January full moon. Marks the Buddha's legendary first visit to the island, when he preached at Mahiyangana; devotees gather at Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara for a major perahera procession.

  • Thu, Jan 15
    Thai Pongalදෙමළ තෛපොංැලල් දිනය

    Thai Pongal (around 14 January) is the main day of the Tamil multi-day harvest festival. It is a public holiday in Sri Lanka, devoted to thanksgiving and prayers to the sun god Surya.

  • Sun, Feb 1
    Nawam Full Moon Poya Dayනවම් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Nawam Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan Buddhist holiday on the February full moon. It commemorates the Buddha's appointment of Sariputta and Moggallana as his chief disciples and the first great assembly of his disciples.

  • Wed, Feb 4
    Independence Dayනිදහස් සමරු දිනය

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Sun, Feb 15
    Maha Shivaratriමහ සිවරාත්රි දිනය

    Maha Sivarathri Day is a public holiday in Sri Lanka honouring the Hindu god Shiva. It falls on the 14th night of the dark fortnight of Phalguna and is marked by fasting and all-night vigils.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    Medin Full Moon Poya Dayමැදින් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Medin Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan Buddhist public holiday observed on the full moon of the lunar month of Medin. It commemorates the Buddha's first visit to his father's palace after attaining enlightenment.

  • Sat, Mar 21
    Eid al-Fitrඊදුල් ෆීතර්

    Eid al-Fitr, the Festival of Breaking the Fast, marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It falls on the first day of Shawwal and is one of the two major Islamic holidays.

  • Wed, Apr 1
    Bak Full Moon Poya Dayබක් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Bak Full Moon Poya Day is a public holiday in Sri Lanka on the full moon of Bak, usually in April. It commemorates the Buddha's second visit to the island, when he is said to have settled a dispute between two Naga kings.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Fridayමහ සිකුරාදා දිනය

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 13
    Day Before Sinhala and Tamil New Yearසිංහල හා දෙමළ අලුත් අවුරුදු දිනට පෙර දිනය

    The Day Before Sinhala and Tamil New Year is a public holiday in Sri Lanka on 12 or 13 April. Families clean their homes and prepare traditional sweets such as kavum and kokis for the new year.

  • Tue, Apr 14
    Sinhala and Tamil New Yearසිංහල හා දෙමළ අලුත් අවුරුදු දිනය

    Sinhala and Tamil New Year is a Sri Lankan holiday on 13-14 April marking the Sinhalese (Aluth Avurudda) and Tamil (Puthandu) new year, when the sun moves from Pisces to Aries; kiribath and family rites are observed.

  • Fri, May 1
    Adhi Vesak Full Moon Poya Dayඅධි වෙසක් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Adhi Vesak Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan public holiday on the full moon of the extra (adhi) Vesak month in the Buddhist lunar calendar. In 2026 it falls on 1 May, while Vesak Poya itself moves to 30 May.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' Dayලොක කම්කරු දිනය

    International Workers' Day, also known as Labour Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working class held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Thu, May 28
    Eid al-Adhaඊදුල් අල්හා

    Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two major Islamic holidays. It honours Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and coincides with the Hajj pilgrimage on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah.

  • Sat, May 30
    Vesak Full Moon Poya Dayවෙසක් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Vesak Poya: Sri Lanka's most important Buddhist holiday, on the May full moon. Commemorates the birth, enlightenment and parinirvana of Gautama Buddha with temple rites, almsgiving, vesak lanterns and dansala food stalls.

  • Sun, May 31
    Day after Vesak Full Moon Poya Dayවෙසක් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනට පසු දිනය

    Sri Lankan public holiday on the day after Vesak Poya, the full-moon day commemorating the Buddha's birth, enlightenment and passing.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Poson Full Moon Poya Dayපොසොන් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Poson Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan public holiday observed on the full moon of June. It commemorates the 3rd century BC arrival of Buddhism in Sri Lanka, brought by Arahat Mahinda from India.

  • Wed, Jul 29
    Esala Full Moon Poya Dayඇසල පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Esala Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan Buddhist public holiday observed on the full moon of the lunar month of Esala. It commemorates the Buddha's first sermon and the procession of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy.

  • Wed, Aug 26
    Prophet Muhammad's Birthdayනබි නායකතුමාගේ උපන් දිනය

    The Prophet's Birthday, known in Arabic as Mawlid an-Nabi, commemorates the birth of Muhammad, the founder of Islam. It is observed on the 12th day of Rabi al-Awwal in the Islamic calendar.

  • Thu, Aug 27
    Nikini Full Moon Poya Dayනිකිණි පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Nikini Full Moon Poya Day is a Buddhist public holiday in Sri Lanka on the full moon of Nikini, usually in August. It commemorates the First Buddhist Council, held after the Buddha's passing.

  • Sat, Sep 26
    Binara Full Moon Poya Dayබිනර පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Sri Lankan Buddhist holiday on the September full moon, commemorating the Buddha's visit to heaven to preach to his mother.

  • Sun, Oct 25
    Vap Full Moon Poya Dayවප් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Vap Poya is a Sri Lankan Buddhist public holiday on the October full moon, marking the end of the three-month Vassa rains retreat and the opening of the Kathina robe-offering season for the monastic community.

  • Sun, Nov 8
    Deepavaliදීපවාලි උත්සව දිනය

    Deepavali Festival Day is a Sri Lankan public holiday for the Hindu festival of lights, also called Diwali. It falls in October or November and is celebrated by Hindu communities with oil lamps, prayers and sweets.

  • Tue, Nov 24
    Il Full Moon Poya Dayඉල් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Il Full Moon Poya Day is a Sri Lankan Buddhist public holiday on the full moon of Il (October–November). It commemorates the Buddha's sending of the first 60 disciples to spread the Dhamma across India.

  • Wed, Dec 23
    Unduvap Full Moon Poya Dayඋඳුවප් පුර පසළොස්වක පෝය දිනය

    Unduvap Full Moon Poya Day is a Buddhist public holiday in Sri Lanka on the full moon of December. It commemorates the arrival of the nun Sanghamitta, who brought a sapling of the Bodhi tree and founded the order of nuns.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Dayනත්තල් උත්සව දිනය

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

🇵🇰 Pakistan Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Pakistan. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

16 public holidays
  • Thu, Feb 5
    Kashmir Solidarity Day

    Kashmir Solidarity Day is a national holiday observed in Pakistan on 5 February each year, on which the country expresses its solidarity with the people of Kashmir. It was first observed in 1990 and is marked by public rallies.

    Eid al-FitrEid-ul-Fitr

    Pakistan Day

    Pakistan Day is a national holiday observed on 23 March. It commemorates the 1940 Lahore Resolution by the All-India Muslim League and the 1956 promulgation of Pakistan's first constitution as a republic.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-AdhaEid-ul-Adha

    Eid-ul-Adha, the Islamic Feast of Sacrifice, is a major public holiday in Pakistan lasting several days from 10 Dhu al-Hijjah. It honours Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and is marked by prayers and animal sacrifice.

  • Thu, May 28
    Eid al-AdhaEid-ul-Adha

  • Thu, May 28
    Youm-e-Takbeer

    Youm-e-Takbeer (Day of Greatness) is observed in Pakistan on 28 May to commemorate the country's first nuclear tests, Chagai-I, conducted in Balochistan in 1998, which made Pakistan a declared nuclear power.

  • Fri, May 29
    Eid al-AdhaEid-ul-Adha

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Ashura

    Ashura is an Islamic holy day observed on the 10th of Muharram. For Sunni Muslims it commemorates the day Moses parted the Red Sea; for Shia Muslims it is a day of mourning for the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali at Karbala in 680 CE.

  • Thu, Jun 25
    Ashura

  • Fri, Aug 14
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Tue, Aug 25
    Eid Milad-un-Nabi

    Eid Milad-un-Nabi commemorates the birth of the Prophet Muhammad and is observed on 12 Rabi al-Awwal in the Islamic calendar. It is a public holiday in Pakistan, and its date is estimated in advance.

  • Mon, Nov 9
    Iqbal Day

    Iqbal Day is a Pakistani public holiday on 9 November, marking the birth of poet-philosopher Allama Muhammad Iqbal (1877-1938), regarded as the spiritual founder of Pakistan. The day features literary events, speeches and recitations.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Quaid-e-Azam Day

    Quaid-e-Azam Day, on 25 December, marks the birthday of Muhammad Ali Jinnah (1876-1948), barrister and founder of Pakistan. Called Quaid-e-Azam (Great Leader), he secured independence on 14 August 1947 and was first Governor-General.

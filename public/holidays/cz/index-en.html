🇨🇿 Czechia Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Czechia. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    Independent Czech State Restoration DayDen obnovy samostatného českého státu

    Independent Czech State Restoration Day is a Czech public holiday observed on 1 January. It commemorates the 1993 dissolution of Czechoslovakia and the simultaneous founding of the independent Czech Republic and Slovak Republic.

  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNový rok

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayVelký pátek

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayVelikonoční pondělí

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DaySvátek práce

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Fri, May 8
    Victory DayDen vítězství

    Victory Day commemorates the end of a war or a decisive military victory. The specific date and conflict marked differ by country, with notable examples including the end of World War II in Europe celebrated on 8 or 9 May.

  • Sun, Jul 5
    Saints Cyril and Methodius DayDen slovanských věrozvěstů Cyrila a Metoděje

    Saints Cyril and Methodius Day honours the 9th-century Byzantine missionaries who brought Christianity and Slavic writing to the Slavs. It is a public holiday on 5 July in Czechia and Slovakia and on 24 May in North Macedonia.

  • Mon, Jul 6
    Jan Hus DayDen upálení mistra Jana Husa

    Jan Hus Day is a Czech public holiday on 6 July, the anniversary of the burning of the reformer Jan Hus (c. 1369–1415) at the Council of Constance after he was condemned as a heretic.

  • Mon, Sep 28
    Statehood DayDen české státnosti

    Statehood Day marks a country's founding or restoration and falls on different dates, such as 15 February in Serbia, 30 May in Croatia, 25 June in Slovenia, 6 July in Lithuania, 13 July in Montenegro and 28 September in Czechia.

  • Wed, Oct 28
    Independent Czechoslovak State DayDen vzniku samostatného československého státu

    Independent Czechoslovak State Day is a Czech public holiday observed on 28 October. It commemorates the 1918 proclamation of an independent Czechoslovak state following the collapse of the Austro-Hungarian Empire.

  • Tue, Nov 17
    Struggle for Freedom and Democracy Day and International Students' DayDen boje za svobodu a demokracii a Mezinárodní den studentstva

    This Czech public holiday on 17 November commemorates the 1939 student protests against the Nazi occupation in Prague and the 1989 Velvet Revolution that ended Communist rule. It is also observed as International Students' Day.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveŠtědrý den

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day1. svátek vánoční

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of Christmas2. svátek vánoční

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

🇻🇮 U.S. Virgin Islands Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in U.S. Virgin Islands. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

20 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    Three Kings Day

    Three Kings Day, observed on 6 January in the US Virgin Islands, is a public holiday marking the Christian feast of Epiphany and the closing day of the Crucian Christmas Festival on St. Croix, with a parade and cultural events.

  • Mon, Jan 19
    Martin Luther King Jr. Day

    Martin Luther King Jr. Day is a United States federal holiday honouring the civil rights leader, observed on the third Monday of January. It marks his birthday on 15 January and was established as a federal holiday in 1983.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Presidents' Day

    Presidents' Day, officially Washington's Birthday, is a US federal holiday on the third Monday of February, also observed in the US Virgin Islands. New Mexico's state government observes it on the day after Thanksgiving.

  • Tue, Mar 31
    Transfer Day

    Transfer Day is a United States Virgin Islands public holiday observed on 31 March. It commemorates the 1917 transfer of the Danish West Indies from Denmark to the United States, when the territory was renamed the U.S. Virgin Islands.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Holy Thursday

    Holy Thursday, also called Maundy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Mon, May 25
    Memorial Day

    Memorial Day is a day of remembrance for those who died in service to their country. In the United States it falls on the last Monday of May; other countries hold similar days on other dates, some also honouring disaster victims.

  • Fri, Jun 19
    JuneteenthJuneteenth National Independence Day

    Juneteenth National Independence Day is a United States federal holiday observed on 19 June to commemorate the emancipation of enslaved African Americans, marking the 1865 announcement of freedom to enslaved people in Texas.

  • Fri, Jul 3
    Emancipation Day

    Emancipation Day commemorates the abolition of slavery. It is observed on different dates in many Caribbean countries and territories and elsewhere in the Americas, each marking its own emancipation history.

  • Fri, Jul 3
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Sat, Jul 4
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Mon, Sep 7
    Labour DayLabor Day

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Columbus Day and Puerto Rico Friendship Day

    Columbus Day and Puerto Rico Friendship Day is a public holiday in the US Virgin Islands on the second Monday of October. It combines Columbus Day with a celebration of ties with neighbouring Puerto Rico.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    Liberty Day

    Liberty Day, also called D. Hamilton Jackson Day, is a public holiday in the U.S. Virgin Islands on 1 November, marking the 1915 founding of The Herald, the territory's first free press, by D. Hamilton Jackson.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Veterans Day

    Veterans Day is a United States federal holiday observed on 11 November to honour military veterans who have served in the United States Armed Forces. It coincides with Armistice Day and Remembrance Day in other countries.

  • Thu, Nov 26
    Thanksgiving Day

    Thanksgiving Day is a holiday for giving thanks for the harvest and the past year's blessings. Each place sets its own date, for example the fourth Thursday of November in US territories and the second Monday of October in Canada.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Christmas Second Day

    Christmas Second Day, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in the U.S. Virgin Islands and corresponds to the day known in other countries as Boxing Day or St. Stephen's Day.

🇳🇫 Norfolk Island Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Norfolk Island. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Jan 26
    Australia Day

    Australia Day, observed on 26 January, is the official national day of Australia. It commemorates the 1788 arrival of the First Fleet at Sydney Cove and the raising of the British flag at that site.

  • Fri, Mar 6
    Foundation Day

    Foundation Day marks the founding of a nation or settlement. Japan observes National Foundation Day on 11 February, and Norfolk Island observes it on 6 March, the anniversary of the first British settlement in 1788.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Sat, Apr 25
    ANZAC Day

    ANZAC Day, observed on 25 April, is a national day of remembrance in Australia and New Zealand honouring members of the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps who served at Gallipoli in 1915 and in all later conflicts.

  • Mon, Jun 8
    Bounty Day

    Bounty Day recalls the mutineers of HMS Bounty and their descendants. Pitcairn marks it on 23 January, when the ship was burned in 1790, and Norfolk Island on 8 June, the arrival of the Pitcairn Islanders in 1856.

  • Mon, Jun 15
    King's Birthday

    The King's Birthday is a public holiday in several monarchies marking the official birthday of the reigning sovereign. The date may differ from the monarch's actual birthday and varies by country.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Show Day

    Show Day is a public holiday on Norfolk Island, usually on the second Monday of October. It marks the island's annual agricultural and horticultural show.

  • Wed, Nov 25
    Thanksgiving Day

    Thanksgiving Day is a holiday for giving thanks for the harvest and the past year's blessings. Each place sets its own date, for example the fourth Thursday of November in US territories and the second Monday of October in Canada.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

  • Mon, Dec 28
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

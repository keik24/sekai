🇵🇾 Paraguay Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Paraguay. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAño Nuevo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Sun, Mar 1
    Heroes' DayDía de los Héroes de la Patria

    Heroes' Day (Día de los Héroes) is a public holiday in Paraguay, usually on 1 March. It marks the death of President Francisco Solano López at Cerro Corá in 1870, at the end of the Paraguayan War.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Maundy ThursdayJueves Santo

    Maundy Thursday, also called Holy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayViernes Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayDomingo de Resurrección

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Fri, May 1
    Workers' DayDía de los Trabajadores

    Workers' Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May, the International Workers' Day, while some nations observe their own date.

  • Thu, May 14
    National HolidayFeriado Nacional

    A public holiday observed nationwide; what it marks differs from country to country. In Japan, for example, a weekday that falls between two national holidays becomes a day off.

  • Fri, May 15
    Independence DayDía de la Independencia Nacional

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Jun 12
    Chaco Armistice DayDía de la Paz del Chaco

    Chaco Armistice Day commemorates the 12 June 1935 ceasefire that ended the Chaco War between Bolivia and Paraguay. It is a public holiday in Paraguay and marks the end of the conflict over the Gran Chaco region.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Founding of AsunciónDía de la Fundación de Asunción

    Asuncion Foundation's Day is a public holiday in Paraguay on 15 August. It commemorates the founding of Asunción, the capital, by Juan de Salazar y Espinosa in 1537.

  • Tue, Sep 29
    Battle of Boquerón DayDía de la Batalla de Boquerón

    Boqueron Battle Day commemorates the Battle of Boquerón, fought between Paraguay and Bolivia from 9 to 29 September 1932 during the Chaco War. The Paraguayan victory at the Bolivian fortín of Boquerón is observed every 29 September.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Virgin of Caacupé DayDía de la Virgen de Caacupé

    Caacupé Virgin Day (Día de la Virgen de Caacupé) is a Paraguayan public holiday on 8 December honouring the Virgin of Caacupé, the country's patroness. Pilgrims gather at the basilica in Caacupé for Paraguay's main Marian feast.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDía de la Navidad

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

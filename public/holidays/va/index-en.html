🇻🇦 Vatican City Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Vatican City. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

29 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    Solemnity of Mary, Mother of GodSolennità di Maria Santissima Madre di Dio

    Solemnity of Mary Mother of God is a public holiday in Vatican City on 1 January. The Roman Catholic feast honours Mary as Theotokos, Mother of God, a title affirmed at the Council of Ephesus in 431, and is marked by a papal Mass.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyEpifania del Signore

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Wed, Feb 11
    Anniversary of the Foundation of Vatican CityAnniversario della istituzione dello Stato della Città del Vaticano

    The Anniversary of the Foundation of Vatican City is a public holiday in Vatican City on 11 February. It marks the signing of the Lateran Treaty in 1929, which established the Vatican City State.

  • Thu, Mar 19
    Saint Joseph's DaySan Giuseppe

    Saint Joseph's Day is the Christian feast honouring Saint Joseph, the husband of Mary and earthly father of Jesus. It is observed on 19 March by Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some other Christian traditions.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Maundy ThursdayGiovedì Santo

    Maundy Thursday, also called Holy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayVenerdì Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Holy SaturdaySabato Santo

    Holy Saturday, also called Black Saturday or Easter Eve, is the day between Good Friday and Easter Sunday in the Christian liturgical calendar. It commemorates the time Jesus' body lay in the tomb.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPasqua di Resurrezione

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayLunedì dell'Angelo

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Tue, Apr 7
    Easter TuesdayMartedì in Albis

    Easter Tuesday is the Tuesday of Easter Week, the week following Easter Sunday in the Christian liturgical calendar. It is observed as a public holiday in a few countries with strong Christian traditions.

  • Fri, May 1
    Saint Joseph the Worker's DaySan Giuseppe Artigiano

    Saint Joseph the Worker's Day is a holiday in Vatican City on 1 May. The Catholic feast, introduced by Pope Pius XII in 1955, honours Saint Joseph as patron of workers.

  • Fri, May 8
    Anniversary of the Election of the Holy FatherAnniversario dell'Elezione del Santo Padre

    The Anniversary of the Election of the Holy Father is a public holiday in the Vatican City State observed on the date each year that the reigning pope was elected by the College of Cardinals.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayAscensione del Signore

    Ascension Day commemorates the Christian belief that Jesus ascended into heaven. It falls on the 40th day of Easter (39 days after Easter Sunday), always a Thursday, and is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, May 24
    PentecostSolennità della Pentecoste

    The Solemnity of Pentecost is a holiday in Vatican City on the Sunday fifty days after Easter. It commemorates the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles.

  • Sun, May 31
    Solemnity of the Holy TrinitySolennità della Santissima Trinità

    The Solemnity of the Holy Trinity is a Catholic feast held on the Sunday after Pentecost. It honours the Trinity of Father, Son and Holy Spirit and is observed in Vatican City.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiCorpus Domini

    Corpus Domini, the Feast of Corpus Christi, is a public holiday in Vatican City. It celebrates the presence of Christ in the Eucharist and falls on the Thursday sixty days after Easter.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saints Peter and Paul's DaySanti Pietro e Paolo

    Saints Peter and Paul's Day, on 29 June, honours the two apostles regarded as founders of the Church in Rome. It is a holiday in Vatican City and in the city of Rome.

  • Fri, Aug 14
    Day before the AssumptionVigilia dell'Assunzione di Maria Santissima

    The Day before the Assumption of Mary is a public holiday in Vatican City on 14 August, the vigil of the Feast of the Assumption on 15 August.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssunzione di Maria Santissima

    The Assumption of Mary is a public holiday in Vatican City on 15 August. The Catholic feast celebrates the belief that Mary was taken body and soul into heaven at the end of her earthly life.

  • Sun, Aug 16
    Day after the AssumptionGiorno Successivo all'Assunzione di Maria Santissima

    The Day after the Assumption of Mary is a public holiday in Vatican City on 16 August, extending the celebration of the Feast of the Assumption on 15 August.

  • Thu, Sep 17
    Name Day of the Holy FatherOnomastico del Santo Padre

    The Name Day of the Holy Father is a holiday in Vatican City on the feast of the pope's baptismal name saint: 23 April (Saint George) for Pope Francis and 17 September (Saint Robert Bellarmine) for Pope Leo XIV.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayTutti i Santi

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' DayTutti i Fedeli Defunti

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionImmacolata Concezione

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveVigilia di Natale

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNatale

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DaySanto Stefano

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

  • Sun, Dec 27
    Saint John the Evangelist's DaySan Giovanni

    Saint John the Evangelist's Day is a holiday in Vatican City on 27 December. It honours the Apostle John, traditionally regarded as the author of the Fourth Gospel.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    Last Day of the YearUltimo giorno dell'anno

    The Last Day of the Year is observed on 31 December, the final day of the Gregorian calendar year. In many countries it is also called New Year's Eve and is marked with celebrations leading into the new year.

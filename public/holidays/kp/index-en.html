🇰🇵 North Korea Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in North Korea. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

22 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day양력설

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Sun, Feb 8
    Korean People's Army Foundation Day조선인민군창건일

    Founding Day of the Korean People's Army is a North Korean holiday on 8 February. It marks the formation of the Korean People's Army on 8 February 1948.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Day of the Shining Star광명성절

    The Day of the Shining Star is a national holiday in North Korea on 16 February, with the following day also off. It celebrates the birthday of the late leader Kim Jong-il.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Day of the Shining Star광명성절

    The Day of the Shining Star is a national holiday in North Korea on 16 February, with the following day also off. It celebrates the birthday of the late leader Kim Jong-il.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Seollal설명절

    Korean New Year, called Seollal, is one of the most important traditional holidays in Korea. It falls on the first day of the lunisolar Korean calendar and is observed over three days with family gatherings and ancestral rites.

  • Tue, Mar 3
    Daeboreum대보름

    Daeboreum (대보름) is a Korean folk holiday on the 15th day of the first lunar month, marking the first full moon of the new lunisolar year. It is a public holiday in the DPRK, observed with traditional foods and folk games.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    International Women's Day국제부녀절

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Cheongmyeong청명

    Cheongmyeong (청명) is a North Korean public holiday observed around 4–6 April on the Qingming solar term. Families visit ancestral graves to perform memorial rites, sweep the tombs and make offerings, mirroring traditions across East Asia.

  • Wed, Apr 15
    Day of the Sun태양절

    The Day of the Sun (Korean: 태양절) is an annual public holiday in North Korea on 15 April, the birth anniversary of Kim Il Sung, founder and Eternal President of North Korea.

  • Thu, Apr 16
    Day of the Sun태양절

    The Day of the Sun (Korean: 태양절) is an annual public holiday in North Korea on 15 April, the birth anniversary of Kim Il Sung, founder and Eternal President of North Korea.

  • Sat, Apr 25
    Korean People's Revolutionary Army Foundation Day조선인민혁명군 창건일

    Founding Day of the Korean People's Revolutionary Army is a North Korean public holiday on 25 April. It marks the date credited with the 1932 founding of an anti-Japanese guerrilla unit led by Kim Il-sung, seen as precursor of the KPA.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' Day전세계근로자들의 국제적명절

    International Workers' Day, also known as Labour Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working class held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Jun 6
    Korean Children's Union Foundation Day조선소년단 창립절

    Foundation Day of the Korean Children's Union is observed in North Korea on 6 June. It marks the 1946 founding of the Korean Children's Union, the country's pioneer organisation for primary-school pupils, with rallies and ceremonies.

  • Mon, Jul 27
    Day of Victory in the Great Fatherland Liberation War조국해방전쟁승리기념일

    The Day of Victory in the Great Fatherland Liberation War is a national holiday in North Korea on 27 July. It marks the signing of the Korean War Armistice Agreement in 1953, which the state celebrates as a victory.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Liberation Day조국해방절

    Liberation Day is a national observance commemorating the day a country was freed from occupation, dictatorship, or foreign rule. The specific date and historical context vary by country.

  • Tue, Aug 25
    Day of Songun선군절

    Day of Songun is a North Korean public holiday observed on 25 August. It commemorates Kim Jong-il's first inspection of a military unit in 1960, which the state holds as the founding date of his military-first policy.

  • Fri, Aug 28
    Youth Day청년절

    Youth Day is a national observance held in many countries to recognise the contributions of young people and to promote youth participation. The specific date and history vary by country.

  • Wed, Sep 9
    Founding Day of the DPRK조선민주주의인민공화국창건일

    Founding Day of the DPRK is a North Korean public holiday on 9 September. It marks the proclamation of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea on 9 September 1948.

  • Fri, Sep 25
    Chuseok추석

    Chuseok is a major Korean harvest festival, observed on the 15th day of the 8th lunar month. Families gather to share food, perform ancestral rites, and give thanks for the autumn harvest.

  • Sat, Oct 10
    Workers' Party of Korea Foundation Day조선로동당창건일

    North Korean public holiday on 10 October marking the 1945 founding of the ruling Workers' Party of Korea. One of the country's major political anniversaries, observed with mass rallies and military parades, especially on milestone years.

  • Mon, Nov 16
    Mother's Day어머니날

    Mother's Day is a celebration honouring mothers, motherhood, and the influence of mothers in society. In most countries it falls on the second Sunday of May, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Sun, Dec 27
    Socialist Constitution Day사회주의헌법절

    Socialist Constitution Day is a public holiday in the Democratic People's Republic of Korea on 27 December, commemorating the adoption in 1972 of the Socialist Constitution of the DPRK, the document that replaced the 1948 constitution.

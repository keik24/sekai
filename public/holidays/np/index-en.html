🇳🇵 Nepal Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Nepal. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

35 public holidays
  • Sun, Jan 11
    Prithvi Jayantiपृथ्वी जयन्ती

    Prithvi Jayanti is a Nepalese public holiday on 11 January (27 Poush). It marks the birth of Prithvi Narayan Shah (1723–1775), king of Gorkha who unified Nepal in the 18th century.

  • Thu, Jan 15
    Maghe Sankrantiमाघे संक्रान्ति

    Maghe Sankranti is a Nepali festival on the first day of Magh in the Vikram Sambat calendar, around mid-January, marking the sun's entry into Capricorn. It is celebrated with ritual bathing, sesame sweets, yam dishes and family gatherings.

  • Mon, Jan 19
    Sonam Lhochharसोनम ल्होछार

    Sonam Lhochhar is the new year festival of the Tamang people of Nepal, observed on the first day of the lunar month of Magh. It marks the start of the Tamang lunar calendar and is a public holiday in Nepal, celebrated with cultural events.

  • Thu, Jan 29
    Martyrs' Dayशहीद दिवस

    Martyrs' Day is observed in Nepal on 16 Magh (late January). It honours those who gave their lives for democracy, notably the four martyrs executed under the Rana regime in 1941.

  • Sun, Feb 15
    Maha Shivaratriमहाशिवरात्रि

    Maha Shivaratri is the Hindu festival honouring the god Shiva, observed annually on the 13th or 14th day of the dark fortnight in the month of Phalguna. Devotees observe fasting and night-long vigils with prayers.

  • Wed, Feb 18
    Gyalpo Lhosarग्याल्पो ल्होसार

    Gyalpo Lhosar is a public holiday in Nepal marking the Tibetan New Year, celebrated mainly by Sherpa and Tibetan communities. It falls in February or March according to the lunisolar calendar.

  • Thu, Feb 19
    National Democracy Dayराष्ट्रिय प्रजातन्त्र दिवस

    National Democracy Day is a public holiday in Nepal on 19 February (7 Falgun). It marks the overthrow of the Rana regime and the establishment of democracy in 1951.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    Fagu Poornimaफागुपूर्णिमा

    Fagu Poornima is Nepal's celebration of Holi, the Hindu festival of colours held on the full moon of Phalguna (February or March). In the hill regions people throw colour powder and water, recall the legend of Prahlada and greet spring.

  • Tue, Mar 3
    Fagu Poornima (Terai)फागुपूर्णिमा (तराई)

    Fagu Poornima (Terai) is the Holi celebration in Nepal's southern Terai plains, observed one day after the hill region's Fagu Poornima. The split reflects ties to neighbouring India, where Holi is celebrated after the full moon of Phalguna.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    International Women's Dayअन्तर्राष्ट्रिय महिला दिवस

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Sat, Mar 21
    Eid al-Fitrईद (ईद उल फित्र) (अनुमानित)

    Eid al-Fitr, the festival marking the end of the Ramadan fast, is a public holiday in Nepal for its Muslim community. Its date follows the Islamic calendar and is estimated in advance.

  • Fri, Mar 27
    Ram Navamiराम नवमी

    Ram Navami is a Hindu festival celebrating the birth of Rama, an incarnation of Vishnu, on the ninth day of the lunar month of Chaitra (March–April). It is a public holiday in India and Nepal.

  • Tue, Apr 14
    Nepali New Yearनेपाली नयाँ वर्ष

    Nepali New Year, known as Naya Barsa, is observed on the first day of Baisakh in the Bikram Sambat calendar, falling in mid-April. It is the official new year of Nepal and is celebrated with cultural events and public processions.

  • Fri, May 1
    Buddha Jayantiबुद्ध जयन्ती

    Buddha Jayanti is a public holiday in Nepal on the full moon of Vaisakha. It commemorates the birth, enlightenment and death of Siddhartha Gautama, who was born in Lumbini in present-day Nepal.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' Dayअन्तर्राष्ट्रिय श्रम दिवस

    International Labor Day, also called International Workers' Day, is a celebration of workers and the labour movement held on 1 May. It is a public holiday in many countries worldwide.

  • Thu, May 28
    Eid al-Adhaबकर ईद (ईद उल अजहा) (अनुमानित)

    Bakr Eid, Eid al-Adha or the Feast of Sacrifice, is a public holiday in Nepal for its Muslim community. Its date follows the Islamic calendar and is estimated in advance.

  • Fri, May 29
    Republic Dayगणतन्त्र दिवस

    Republic Day is a national holiday observed by many countries to commemorate the founding of their republic or the adoption of a republican form of government. The specific date varies by country.

  • Fri, Aug 28
    Janai Poornimaजनै पूर्णिमा

    Janai Poornima is a Hindu festival on the full moon of Shravan, when Hindu men replace their sacred thread (janai) and Raksha Bandhan is observed. Newar Buddhists mark the day as Gunhu Punhi. It is a public holiday in Nepal.

  • Fri, Sep 4
    Krishna Janmashtamiश्रीकृष्ण जन्माष्टमी

    Shree Krishna Janmashtami is a Nepalese public holiday marking the Hindu festival of the birth of Lord Krishna, eighth avatar of Vishnu. Also called Janma Ashtami, it falls on the 8th day of Bhadra's dark fortnight (Aug-Sep).

  • Sat, Sep 19
    Constitution Dayसंविधान दिवस

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Sun, Oct 11
    Ghatasthapanaघटस्थापना

    Ghatasthapana is the first day of Nepal's Dashain festival and a public holiday. A sacred vessel of water is set up and barley seeds are sown to grow jamara, which is later given as a blessing.

  • Sun, Oct 18
    Fulpatiफुलपाती

    Fulpati is the seventh day of Nepal's Dashain festival and a public holiday. A sacred bundle of flowers, leaves and plants is carried in procession to Hanuman Dhoka in Kathmandu.

  • Mon, Oct 19
    Maha Ashtamiमहा अष्टमी

    Maha Ashtami, also called Durga Ashtami, is a public holiday in Nepal on the eighth day of the Dashain festival. It is devoted to the goddess Durga and marked with special worship and offerings.

  • Mon, Oct 19
    Maha Navamiमहा नवमी

    Maha Navami is a public holiday in Nepal on the ninth day of the Dashain festival, in the lunar month of Ashwin. It honours the goddess Durga and her victory over the demon Mahishasura.

  • Tue, Oct 20
    Bijaya Dashamiविजया दशमी

    Bijaya Dashami is a public holiday in Nepal on the tenth day of Dashain, celebrating the goddess Durga's victory over evil. Elders give red tika and blessings to younger family members.

  • Wed, Oct 21
    Ekadashi (Dashain)एकादशी (दशैं)

    Ekadashi is the day after Vijaya Dashami during Nepal's Dashain festival, observed as part of the Dashain public holidays. Families continue visiting elders to receive tika and blessings.

  • Thu, Oct 22
    Duwadashi (Dashain)दुवादशी (दशैं)

    The 12th day of Nepal's Dashain festival, when families visit elders to receive tika and jamara blessings.

  • Sun, Nov 8
    Laxmi Poojaलक्ष्मीपूजा

    Laxmi Pooja is a public holiday in Nepal during Tihar, the festival of lights, devoted to Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and prosperity. Homes are cleaned, decorated and lit with lamps to welcome her blessings.

  • Mon, Nov 9
    Gai Tiharगाई तिहार

    Gai Tihar is the third day of Nepal's Tihar festival of lights, coinciding with Laxmi Puja, when devotees honour the cow as a sacred symbol of wealth and motherhood. Cows are garlanded with marigolds and fed special meals.

  • Tue, Nov 10
    Gobardhan Poojaगोवर्धन पूजा

    Gobardhan Pooja is the fourth day of Nepal's Tihar festival of lights. Hindu families build small mounds of cow dung representing Govardhan Hill, which Krishna lifted to shelter Vrindavan from the torrential rain sent by the god Indra.

  • Tue, Nov 10
    Mha Pujaम्ह पूजा

    Mha Pooja is a public holiday in Nepal during Tihar, when Newars perform a ritual worship of the self. It falls on the first day of the bright fortnight of Kartik, which is also Nepal Sambat New Year.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Bhai Tikaभाइटीका

    Bhai Tika is a public holiday in Nepal on the last day of the Tihar festival. Sisters place a tika on their brothers' foreheads and pray for their long life, and gifts are exchanged.

  • Sun, Nov 15
    Chhath Parvaछठ पर्व

    Chhath Parva is a public holiday in Nepal for the Hindu festival of Chhath, widely observed in the southern plains. Devotees fast and offer prayers to the sun god Surya at sunset and sunrise by rivers and ponds.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Dayक्रिसमसको दिन

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Wed, Dec 30
    Tamu Lhochharतमु ल्हाेसार

    Tamu Lhochhar is the new-year festival of Nepal's Gurung community, held on Poush 15 in the Nepali calendar, around 30 December. It is a public holiday in Nepal.

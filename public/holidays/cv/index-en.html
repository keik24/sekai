🇨🇻 Cape Verde Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Cape Verde. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

12 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAno Novo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 13
    Democracy and Freedom DayDia da Liberdade e da Democracia

    Democracy and Freedom Day is a Cape Verdean public holiday on 13 January. It commemorates the country's first multiparty elections, held on 13 January 1991, which marked the transition from one-party rule to democracy.

  • Thu, Jan 15
    Tarrafal Municipality DayDia do Município do Tarrafal de SantiagoRegional

    A municipal holiday in Tarrafal, on the island of Santiago in Cape Verde, held on 15 January.

  • Sat, Jan 17
    Santo Antão Island DayDia da Ilha de Santo AntãoRegional

    A holiday on the island of Santo Antão in Cape Verde on 17 January, the feast of Saint Anthony the Abbot, after whom the island is named. It is observed in all three of its municipalities.

  • Tue, Jan 20
    National Heroes' DayDia da Nacionalidade e dos Heróis Nacionais

    National Heroes Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour individuals who have contributed to their nation's independence, freedom, or development. The specific date and honourees vary by country.

  • Thu, Jan 22
    São Vicente Municipality DayDia do Município de São VicenteRegional

    A municipal holiday on the island of São Vicente in Cape Verde on 22 January, the feast of its patron, Saint Vincent.

  • Sat, Jan 31
    Ribeira Grande de Santiago Municipality DayDia do Município de Ribeira Grande de SantiagoRegional

    A municipal holiday in Ribeira Grande de Santiago, Cabo Verde, the municipality around Cidade Velha, the first European colonial town in the tropics.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Carnival TuesdayTerça-feira de CarnavalRegional

    Carnival Tuesday is the last day of carnival and a public holiday in several countries. Where carnival precedes Lent it is Shrove Tuesday, the day before Ash Wednesday, while some Caribbean islands hold it in summer.

  • Wed, Feb 18
    Ash WednesdayQuarta-feira de Cinzas

    Ash Wednesday is a Christian holy day of prayer and fasting that marks the first day of Lent, the forty-day period preceding Easter. It is observed by Roman Catholics, Anglicans, Lutherans, Methodists and many other Western Christians.

  • Fri, Mar 13
    São Domingos Municipality DayDia do Município de São DomingosRegional

    The municipal holiday of São Domingos, a rural municipality on the island of Santiago, Cabo Verde, observed on 13 March.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridaySexta-feira Santa

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPáscoa

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Fri, May 1
    Workers' DayDia do Trabalhador

    Worker's Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May, the International Workers' Day, while some nations observe their own date.

  • Fri, May 1
    São Filipe Municipality DayDia do Município de São FilipeRegional

    A municipal holiday in São Filipe on the island of Fogo, Cape Verde, on 1 May, the feast of the town's patron, Saint Philip.

  • Thu, May 7
    Ribeira Grande Municipality DayDia do Município de Ribeira GrandeRegional

    A municipal holiday in Ribeira Grande on the island of Santo Antão, Cape Verde, held on 7 May.

  • Sat, May 9
    São Lourenço dos Órgãos Municipality DayDia do Município de São Lourenço dos ÓrgãosRegional

    A municipal holiday in São Lourenço dos Órgãos, Cabo Verde, on May 9, marking the municipality's creation in 2005 from part of Santa Cruz.

  • Tue, May 19
    Praia Municipality DayDia do Município da PraiaRegional

    A municipal holiday in Praia, the capital of Cape Verde, on 19 May.

  • Mon, Jun 1
    International Children's DayDia Mundial da Criança

    International Children's Day, observed on 1 June in many countries, is a celebration honouring children and promoting their welfare. It originated in the 1925 World Conference for the Wellbeing of Children in Geneva.

  • Sat, Jun 13
    Paúl Municipality DayDia do Município do PaúlRegional

    A municipal holiday in Paúl, on the island of Santo Antão in Cape Verde, held on 13 June, the feast of Saint Anthony.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Brava Municipality DayDia do Município da BravaRegional

  • Sat, Jul 4
    Boa Vista Municipality DayDia do Município da Boa VistaRegional

  • Sun, Jul 5
    Independence DayDia da Independência Nacional

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Sun, Jul 19
    São Salvador do Mundo Municipality DayDia do Município de São Salvador do MundoRegional

  • Sat, Jul 25
    Santa Cruz Municipality DayDia do Município de Santa CruzRegional

  • Sun, Aug 2

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryDia da Assunção

  • Sat, Aug 15

  • Wed, Sep 2

  • Tue, Sep 15
    Sal Municipality DayDia do Município do SalRegional

    A municipal holiday on the island of Sal in Cape Verde, held on 15 September.

  • Tue, Sep 29
    São Miguel Municipality DayDia do Município de São MiguelRegional

    A municipal holiday in São Miguel, on the island of Santiago in Cape Verde, held on 29 September, the feast of Saint Michael the Archangel.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayDia de Todos os Santos

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Wed, Nov 25
    Santa Catarina Municipality DayDia do Município de Santa Catarina de SantiagoRegional

    A municipal holiday in Santa Catarina on the island of Santiago, Cape Verde, on 25 November, the feast of Saint Catherine.

  • Wed, Nov 25
    Santa Catarina do Fogo Municipality DayDia do Município de Santa Catarina do FogoRegional

  • Sun, Dec 6
    Ribeira Brava Municipality DayDia do Município de Ribeira BravaRegional

    The municipal holiday of Ribeira Brava on São Nicolau, Cabo Verde, on 6 December, the feast of Saint Nicholas, the island's patron and traditional date of its discovery.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDia do Natal

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

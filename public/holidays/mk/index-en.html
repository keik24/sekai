🇲🇰 North Macedonia Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in North Macedonia. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayНова Година

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Wed, Jan 7
    Christmas DayБожиќ

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-FitrРамазан Бајрам (проценето)

    Ramazan Bajram, Eid al-Fitr, is a public holiday in North Macedonia marking the end of Ramadan with prayer and family visits. Its date follows the Islamic calendar and is estimated in advance.

  • Mon, Apr 13
    Easter MondayВелигден

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayДен на трудот

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Sun, May 24
    Saints Cyril and Methodius DayСветите Кирил и Методиј - Ден на сесловенските просветители

    Saints Cyril and Methodius Day honours the 9th-century Byzantine missionaries who brought Christianity and Slavic writing to the Slavs. It is a public holiday on 5 July in Czechia and Slovakia and on 24 May in North Macedonia.

  • Mon, May 25
    Светите Кирил и Методиј - Ден на сесловенските просветители (неработен ден)
  • Sun, Aug 2
    Republic DayДен на Републиката

    Republic Day is a national holiday observed by many countries to commemorate the founding of their republic or the adoption of a republican form of government. The specific date varies by country.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    Ден на Републиката (неработен ден)
  • Tue, Sep 8
    Independence DayДен на независноста

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Sun, Oct 11
    National Uprising DayДен на народното востание

    National Uprising Day is a public holiday in North Macedonia on 11 October. It marks the start of the 1941 uprising against the Axis occupation during the Second World War.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Ден на народното востание (неработен ден)
  • Fri, Oct 23
    Macedonian Revolutionary Struggle DayДен на македонската револуционерна борба

    Macedonian Revolutionary Struggle Day is a public holiday in North Macedonia on 23 October. It marks the founding in 1893 of the Internal Macedonian Revolutionary Organization, which fought against Ottoman rule.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Saint Clement of Ohrid DayСвети Климент Охридски

    Saint Clement of Ohrid Day is a North Macedonian public holiday observed on 8 December. It honours Saint Clement of Ohrid (840–916), a disciple of Saints Cyril and Methodius who developed the Cyrillic alphabet.

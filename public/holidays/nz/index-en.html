🇳🇿 New Zealand Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in New Zealand. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Day after New Year's Day

    The Day after New Year's Day is a public holiday on 2 January in several countries, extending the New Year celebration into a second day off.

  • Mon, Jan 19
    Wellington Anniversary DayRegional

    The provincial anniversary holiday of Wellington, New Zealand, marking the arrival of the first settlers on 22 January 1840; it is observed on the Monday nearest that date.

  • Mon, Jan 26
    Auckland Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in New Zealand's Auckland and Northland regions, held on the Monday nearest 29 January. It marks the founding of the Auckland province and the arrival of Governor Hobson in 1840.

  • Mon, Feb 2
    Nelson Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in Nelson, New Zealand, marking the founding of the settlement; the anniversary is 1 February and the holiday is observed on the nearest Monday.

  • Fri, Feb 6
    Waitangi Day

    Waitangi Day is New Zealand's national day, on 6 February. It marks the signing in 1840 of the Treaty of Waitangi between Māori chiefs and the British Crown, considered the country's founding document. Ceremonies are held at Waitangi.

  • Mon, Mar 9
    Taranaki Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional anniversary holiday in Taranaki, New Zealand, observed on the second Monday of March.

  • Mon, Mar 23
    Otago Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in the Otago region of New Zealand, marking the arrival of the first Scottish settlers at Port Chalmers on 23 March 1848. It is observed on the Monday nearest that date.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Tue, Apr 7
    Southland Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional anniversary holiday in Southland, New Zealand, observed on Easter Tuesday.

  • Sat, Apr 25
    Anzac Day

    Anzac Day, observed on 25 April, is a national day of remembrance in Australia and New Zealand honouring the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps who served at Gallipoli in 1915 and in all later military conflicts.

  • Mon, Apr 27
    Anzac Day

    Anzac Day, observed on 25 April, is a national day of remembrance in Australia and New Zealand honouring the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps who served at Gallipoli in 1915 and in all later military conflicts.

  • Mon, Jun 1
    King's Birthday

    The King's Birthday is a public holiday in several monarchies marking the official birthday of the reigning sovereign. The date may differ from the monarch's actual birthday and varies by country.

  • Fri, Jul 10
    Matariki

    Matariki is a public holiday in New Zealand marking the Māori New Year, signalled by the rising of the Matariki star cluster (Pleiades). It falls on a Friday in June or July, set in advance according to the Māori lunar calendar.

  • Mon, Sep 28
    South Canterbury Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in New Zealand's South Canterbury, held on the fourth Monday of September, the date of the former Dominion Day.

  • Fri, Oct 23
    Hawke's Bay Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional anniversary holiday in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand, observed on the Friday before Labour Day in October.

  • Mon, Oct 26
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    Marlborough Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in the Marlborough region of New Zealand, marking the founding of Marlborough Province on 1 November. It is observed on the first Monday after Labour Day.

  • Fri, Nov 13
    Canterbury Anniversary DayRegional

    The provincial anniversary holiday of Canterbury, New Zealand, observed in Christchurch on the second Friday after the first Tuesday of November to coincide with the Canterbury A&P Show.

  • Mon, Nov 30
    Chatham Islands Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in New Zealand's Chatham Islands, held on the Monday nearest 30 November, the anniversary of the islands' foundation as part of the colony.

  • Mon, Nov 30
    West Coast Anniversary DayRegional

    A regional holiday in the West Coast region of New Zealand, marking the region's anniversary on 1 December. It is observed on the Monday nearest that date.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

  • Mon, Dec 28

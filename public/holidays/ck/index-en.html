🇨🇰 Cook Islands Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Cook Islands. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

12 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Day after New Year's Day

    The Day after New Year's Day is a public holiday on 2 January in several countries, extending the New Year celebration into a second day off.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Sat, Apr 25
    Anzac Day

    Anzac Day, observed on 25 April, is a national day of remembrance in Australia and New Zealand honouring the Australian and New Zealand Army Corps who served at Gallipoli in 1915 and in all later military conflicts.

  • Mon, Jun 1
    Sovereign's Birthday

    Sovereign's Birthday marks the official birthday of the British monarch. It is a public holiday in the Cook Islands on the first Monday of June and in the British Virgin Islands on a Friday in June.

  • Fri, Jul 3
    Ra o te Ui Ariki

    Ra o te Ui Ariki is the Day of the Ariki, observed in the Cook Islands on the first Friday of July. It honours the traditional chiefs (Ariki) who serve in the House of Ariki, a consultative body advising parliament on matters of tradition.

  • Tue, Aug 4
    Constitution Day

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Mon, Oct 26
    Cook Islands Gospel Day

    Cook Islands Gospel Day is a public holiday on 26 October. It marks the arrival of Christianity on Aitutaki in 1821 through missionaries of the London Missionary Society, and is celebrated with church services and pageants.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

  • Mon, Dec 28
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

🇮🇷 Iran Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Iran. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

28 public holidays
  • Sat, Jan 3
    Birthday of Imam Ali and Father's Dayولادت امام علی علیه السلام و روز پدر

    This Iranian public holiday on 13 Rajab jointly celebrates the birthday of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib, the first Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam, and Father's Day. Imam Ali is considered the model father in the Iranian Shi'a tradition.

  • Sat, Jan 17
    Mab'athمبعث رسول اکرم (ص)

    This Iranian and Shi'a Muslim observance commemorates the start of the prophetic mission of Muhammad. It is observed on 27 Rajab and marks the date on which the Prophet first received divine revelation through the angel Gabriel.

  • Wed, Feb 4
    Birth of Imam Mahdi and Mid-Sha'banولادت حضرت قائم عجل الله تعالی فرجه و جشن نیمه شعبان

    This Iranian public holiday on 15 Shaban commemorates the birth of Muhammad al-Mahdi, the twelfth and final Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam. The day is also marked with night-time festivities known as Mid-Shaban.

  • Wed, Feb 11
    Islamic Revolution Dayپیروزی انقلاب اسلامی

    Islamic Revolution Day is an Iranian national holiday on 22 Bahman (10 or 11 February). It marks the victory of the 1979 Iranian Revolution, which ended the Pahlavi monarchy.

  • Wed, Mar 11
    Martyrdom of Imam Aliشهادت حضرت علی علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 21 Ramadan commemorates the martyrdom of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (601–661 CE), the cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad, the fourth caliph, and the first Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Iranian Oil Industry Nationalization Dayروز ملی شدن صنعت نفت ایران

    Iranian Oil Industry Nationalization Day is an Iranian holiday on 29 Esfand (19 or 20 March). It commemorates the 1951 law under Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh that nationalised the country's oil industry.

  • Sat, Mar 21
    Eid al-Fitrعید سعید فطر

    This Iranian observance is Eid al-Fitr, the Islamic festival marking the end of the Ramadan fast. It is celebrated on 1 Shawwal with communal prayers, charity to the poor, family gatherings, and festive meals.

  • Sat, Mar 21
    Nowruzجشن نوروز

    Nowruz is the Persian New Year, marked at the spring equinox around 20–21 March. It is a public holiday in several countries, including Iran, Iraq and Uzbekistan, and is celebrated widely across the region.

  • Sun, Mar 22
    Eid al-Fitr Holidayتعطیل به مناسبت عید سعید فطر

    This Iranian public holiday observes the additional day off around Eid al-Fitr, the Islamic festival marking the end of the Ramadan fast. Iran observes a multi-day break starting on 1 Shawwal in the Islamic calendar.

  • Sun, Mar 22
    Nowruz Holidayعیدنوروز

    The Nowruz Holiday covers the additional days off around Nowruz, the New Year celebrated at the spring equinox around 21 March, in countries such as Iran and Kazakhstan.

  • Mon, Mar 23
    Nowruz Holidayعیدنوروز

    The Nowruz Holiday covers the additional days off around Nowruz, the New Year celebrated at the spring equinox around 21 March, in countries such as Iran and Kazakhstan.

  • Tue, Mar 24
    Nowruz Holidayعیدنوروز

    The Nowruz Holiday covers the additional days off around Nowruz, the New Year celebrated at the spring equinox around 21 March, in countries such as Iran and Kazakhstan.

  • Wed, Apr 1
    Islamic Republic Dayروز جمهوری اسلامی

    Islamic Republic Day is an Iranian public holiday on 12 Farvardin (31 March or 1 April). It commemorates the referendum of March 1979 in which voters approved the Islamic Republic as Iran's form of government.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Nature's Dayروز طبیعت

    Nature's Day, known as Sizdah Bedar, is an Iranian celebration on the thirteenth day of Nowruz, which falls on 1 or 2 April. Iranians traditionally spend the day outdoors with family, picnicking in parks and the countryside.

  • Tue, Apr 14
    Martyrdom of Imam Ja'far al-Sadiqشهادت امام جعفر صادق علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 25 Shawwal commemorates the martyrdom of Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (702–765 CE), the sixth Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam and founder of the Jafari school of Islamic jurisprudence.

  • Thu, May 28
    Eid al-Adhaعید سعید قربان

    This Iranian observance is Eid al-Adha, the Islamic Feast of Sacrifice. Falling on 10 Dhu al-Hijjah, it commemorates Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and is honoured with prayers and the ritual slaughter of livestock.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Death of Imam Khomeiniرحلت حضرت امام خمینی

    Death of Imam Khomeini is an Iranian public holiday on 14 Khordad (3 or 4 June). It marks the 1989 death of Ruhollah Khomeini, leader of the 1979 Islamic Revolution and the first Supreme Leader of Iran.

  • Fri, Jun 5
    15 Khordad Uprisingقیام 15 خرداد

    The 15 Khordad Uprising commemorates the demonstrations of 5 and 6 June 1963 in Iran, which protested the arrest of Ayatollah Khomeini after his denouncement of the Shah and Israel. It is observed annually in Iran.

  • Fri, Jun 5
    Eid al-Ghadirعید سعید غدیر خم

    This Iranian and Shi'a Muslim observance is Eid al-Ghadir, commemorating the Prophet Muhammad's sermon at Ghadir Khumm in 632 CE. It is observed on 18 Dhu al-Hijjah and is regarded as the appointment of Ali as his successor.

  • Thu, Jun 25
    Tasuaتاسوعای حسینی

    This Iranian observance is Tasua, the ninth day of Muharram. It commemorates the events leading to the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali at the Battle of Karbala in 680 CE and is a public holiday in Iran.

  • Fri, Jun 26
    Ashuraعاشورای حسینی

    This Iranian and Shi'a Muslim observance is Ashura, the tenth day of Muharram. It commemorates the 680 CE martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali, grandson of the Prophet Muhammad, at the Battle of Karbala and is a day of mourning.

  • Tue, Aug 4
    Arbaeenاربعین حسینی

    Arbaeen is a public holiday in Iran marking the fortieth day after the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali at Karbala in 680 CE. It falls on 20 Safar, and many pilgrims travel to Karbala in Iraq.

  • Wed, Aug 12
    Demise of the Prophet and Martyrdom of Imam Hasanرحلت رسول اکرم؛شهادت امام حسن مجتبی علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 28 Safar jointly observes the death of the Prophet Muhammad in 632 CE and the martyrdom of his grandson Hasan ibn Ali, the second Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam.

  • Fri, Aug 14
    Martyrdom of Ali al-Ridaشهادت امام رضا علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 30 Safar commemorates the martyrdom of Imam Ali al-Rida (765–818 CE), the eighth Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam. His tomb in Mashhad is one of the holiest sites in Shi'a Islam.

  • Sat, Aug 22
    Martyrdom of Hasan al-Askariشهادت امام حسن عسکری علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 8 Rabi al-Awwal commemorates the martyrdom of Imam Hasan al-Askari (846–874 CE), the eleventh Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam and father of Muhammad al-Mahdi, the awaited final Imam.

  • Mon, Aug 31
    Birthday of the Prophet and Imam Ja'far al-Sadiqمیلاد رسول اکرم و امام جعفر صادق علیه السلام

    This Iranian public holiday on 17 Rabi al-Awwal jointly commemorates the birthdays of the Prophet Muhammad and Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (702–765 CE), the sixth Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam.

  • Sat, Nov 14
    Martyrdom of Fatimaشهادت حضرت فاطمه زهرا سلام الله علیها

    This Iranian observance commemorates the martyrdom of Fatima al-Zahra (605–632 CE), the daughter of the Prophet Muhammad and wife of Ali ibn Abi Talib. The date is observed on 3 Jumada al-Thani in the Islamic calendar.

  • Wed, Dec 23
    Birthday of Imam Ali and Father's Dayولادت امام علی علیه السلام و روز پدر

    This Iranian public holiday on 13 Rajab jointly celebrates the birthday of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib, the first Imam of Twelver Shi'a Islam, and Father's Day. Imam Ali is considered the model father in the Iranian Shi'a tradition.

🇨🇭 Switzerland Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Switzerland. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

4 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNeujahrstag

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Saint Berchtold's DayBerchtoldstagRegional

    Saint Berchtold's Day (Berchtoldstag) is a holiday in many Swiss cantons on 2 January, extending the New Year celebrations. Its origins are uncertain.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyHeilige Drei KönigeRegional

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Sun, Mar 1
    Republic DayJahrestag der Ausrufung der RepublikRegional

    Republic Day is a national holiday observed by many countries to commemorate the founding of their republic or the adoption of a republican form of government. The specific date varies by country.

  • Thu, Mar 19
    Saint Joseph's DayJosefstagRegional

    Saint Joseph's Day is the Christian feast honouring Saint Joseph, the husband of Mary and earthly father of Jesus. It is observed on 19 March by Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some other Christian traditions.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayKarfreitagRegional

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayOstermontagRegional

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Thu, Apr 9
    Näfelser FahrtRegional

    A public holiday in the Swiss canton of Glarus on the first Thursday of April. It commemorates the Battle of Näfels of 1388, in which Glarus defeated Habsburg forces, with a pilgrimage to the battlefield.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayTag der ArbeitRegional

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayAuffahrt

    Ascension Day commemorates the Christian belief that Jesus ascended into heaven. It falls on the 40th day of Easter (39 days after Easter Sunday), always a Thursday, and is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, May 25
    Whit MondayPfingstmontagRegional

    Whit Monday, also called Pentecost Monday, is the Christian holiday on the day after Pentecost, the seventh Sunday after Easter. It is a public holiday in many countries in Europe, Africa and the Caribbean.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiFronleichnamRegional

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Tue, Jun 23
    Independence DayFest der UnabhängigkeitRegional

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saints Peter and PaulPeter und PaulRegional

    A public holiday in the Swiss canton of Ticino on 29 June, the feast of the apostles Peter and Paul.

  • Sat, Aug 1
    National DayNationalfeiertag

    National Day is the designated date on which a country celebrates its nationhood, often marking independence, unification, the founding of a republic, or the birthday of a sovereign. The date varies by country.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryMariä HimmelfahrtRegional

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Thu, Sep 10
    Jeûne genevoisGenfer BettagRegional

    A public holiday in the Swiss canton of Geneva, held on the Thursday after the first Sunday of September; it is a traditional day of fasting and prayer.

  • Sat, Sep 12
    Knabenschiessen (Zurich)KnabenschiessenRegional

    A traditional shooting contest and fair for young people in Zurich, Switzerland, held over the second weekend of September; the Monday afternoon is a customary half-holiday in the city.

  • Sun, Sep 13
    Knabenschiessen (Zurich)KnabenschiessenRegional

    A traditional shooting contest and fair for young people in Zurich, Switzerland, held over the second weekend of September; the Monday afternoon is a customary half-holiday in the city.

  • Mon, Sep 21
    Federal Fast Monday (Vaud)BettagsmontagRegional

    Lundi du Jeûne, a holiday in the Swiss canton of Vaud on the Monday after the Federal Day of Thanksgiving, Repentance and Prayer (third Sunday of September).

  • Fri, Sep 25
    Brother Klaus (Saint Nicholas of Flüe)Bruder KlausRegional

    A public holiday in the Swiss canton of Obwalden on 25 September, the feast of Saint Nicholas of Flüe, the 15th-century hermit known as Brother Klaus and patron saint of Switzerland.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayAllerheiligenRegional

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionMariä EmpfängnisRegional

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayWeihnachten

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DayStephanstagRegional

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    Restoration DayWiederherstellung der RepublikRegional

    Restoration Day is a holiday in the Dominican Republic on 16 August, marking the 1863 start of the war that restored independence from Spain, and in the Swiss canton of Geneva on 31 December, marking the restoration of the republic in 1813.

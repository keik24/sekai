🇲🇺 Mauritius Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Mauritius. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

15 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Day after New Year's Day

    The Day after New Year's Day is a public holiday on 2 January in several countries, extending the New Year celebration into a second day off.

  • Sun, Feb 1
    Abolition of Slavery

    The Abolition of Slavery holiday commemorates the end of slavery. Mauritius observes it on 1 February, while French overseas regions mark it on their own dates in April, May, June or December.

  • Mon, Feb 2
    Thaipoosam Cavadee

    Thaipoosam Cavadee is a Hindu Tamil festival observed in Mauritius and other Tamil diaspora communities. Held on the full moon of the lunar month of Thai, it honours the god Murugan with kavadi processions and acts of devotional sacrifice.

  • Sun, Feb 15
    Maha ShivaratriMaha Shivaratree

    Maha Shivaratree, the Great Night of Shiva, is a Hindu festival in honour of the deity Shiva. It is observed on the 14th night of the dark fortnight of the Hindu month of Phalguna and is a public holiday in Mauritius.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Spring FestivalChinese Spring Festival

    The Chinese Spring Festival, the first day of the lunisolar calendar year, is a public holiday in Mauritius. It is celebrated by the Sino-Mauritian community with family gatherings and festivities.

  • Thu, Mar 12
    Independence and Republic Day

    Independence and Republic Day marks both independence and the move to a republic. Mauritius observes it on 12 March (independence 1968, republic 1992), and Tanzania on 9 December (Tanganyika's independence in 1961 and republic in 1962).

  • Thu, Mar 19
    UgadiOugadi

    Ugadi is the Telugu and Kannada New Year festival, observed on the first day of the lunar month of Chaitra in the Hindu calendar. It is a public holiday in Mauritius, celebrated by the Hindu community.

  • Sat, Mar 21
    Eid al-FitrEid-ul-Fitr

    Eid-ul-Fitr, the Festival of Breaking the Fast, marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It falls on the first day of Shawwal and is one of the two major Islamic holidays.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary

    The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary is a public holiday in Mauritius on 15 August, observed in alternate years. The Christian feast marks the belief that Mary was taken bodily into heaven.

  • Mon, Sep 14
    Ganesh Chaturthi

    Ganesh Chaturthi is a public holiday in Mauritius celebrating the birth of Ganesha, the elephant-headed Hindu deity. It falls in the Hindu month of Bhadrapada, usually in August or September.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    Arrival of Indentured Labourers

    Arrival of Indentured Labourers is a public holiday in Mauritius observed on 2 November. It commemorates the arrival of the first indentured workers from India in 1834, who replaced enslaved labour on the island's sugar estates.

  • Sun, Nov 8
    Divali

    Divali, also called Diwali or Deepavali, is the Hindu festival of lights, symbolising the victory of light over darkness. It is a public holiday in several countries with Hindu communities and falls in October or November.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

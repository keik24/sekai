🇺🇬 Uganda Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Uganda. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Jan 26
    NRM Liberation Day

    NRM Liberation Day is a Ugandan public holiday on 26 January. It commemorates the 1986 entry into Kampala by the National Resistance Army, the armed wing of the National Resistance Movement, which brought Yoweri Museveni to power.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Archbishop Janani Luwum Day

    Archbishop Janani Luwum Day is a public holiday in Uganda on 16 February. It honours Janani Luwum, Anglican Archbishop of Uganda, who was killed in 1977 under the regime of Idi Amin.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    Women's Day

    Women's Day is an observance celebrating the achievements of women and advocating gender equality. In many countries it coincides with International Women's Day on 8 March; some nations mark their own national women's day.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-Fitr

    Eid al-Fitr, the Festival of Breaking the Fast, marks the end of Ramadan, the Islamic holy month of fasting. It falls on the first day of Shawwal and is one of the two major Islamic holidays.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour Day

    Labour Day honours workers and the labour movement. Most countries mark it on 1 May, while Canada and Bermuda use the first Monday of September and others, including Australia and New Zealand, set their own dates.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-Adha

    Eid al-Adha, the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two major Islamic holidays. It honours Ibrahim's willingness to sacrifice his son and coincides with the Hajj pilgrimage on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah.

  • Wed, Jun 3
    Uganda Martyrs' Day

    Uganda Martyrs' Day is a public holiday in Uganda on 3 June. It honours the Catholic and Anglican converts killed under King Mwanga II in 1885–1887, and many pilgrims gather at Namugongo.

  • Tue, Jun 9
    National Heroes' Day

    National Heroes' Day is a national observance held in several countries to honour individuals recognised for outstanding contributions to their nation. The specific date and honourees vary by country.

  • Fri, Oct 9
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

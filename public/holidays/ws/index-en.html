🇼🇸 Samoa Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Samoa. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

11 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Day after New Year's DayThe Day After New Year's Day

    The Day After New Year's Day is a Samoan public holiday observed on 2 January. It extends the New Year celebrations and reflects the broader Pacific island tradition of marking the first days of the year as festive holidays.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Day After Good Friday

    The Day After Good Friday, or Holy Saturday, falls between Good Friday and Easter Sunday. It is a day of silent vigil at the tomb of Christ and is observed as a public holiday in Samoa.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Mon, May 11
    Mother's Day

    Mother's Day is a celebration honouring mothers, motherhood, and the influence of mothers in society. In most countries it falls on the second Sunday of May, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Mon, Jun 1
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Mon, Aug 10
    Father's Day

    Father's Day is a celebration honouring fathers and fatherhood. In many countries it falls on the third Sunday of June, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    White SundayWhite Sunday (Lotu a Tamaiti)

    In Samoa, White Sunday (Lotu a Tamaiti) falls on the second Sunday of October, and the following Monday is a public holiday. Children dressed in white lead church services and are honoured by their families.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing Day

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

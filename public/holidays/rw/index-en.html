    Eid al-FitrEid El Fitr (yagereranijwe)

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayUmunsi wa Gatanu Mutagatifu

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayKu wa mbere wa Pasika

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Tue, Apr 7
    Genocide against the Tutsi Memorial DayUmunsi wo Kwibuka Jenoside yakorewe Abatutsi mu 1994

    Rwandan day of remembrance on 7 April for victims of the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi, in which an estimated 800,000 Tutsi and moderate Hutu were killed by Hutu extremists and Interahamwe militia over about one hundred days.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayUmunsi Mukuru w'Umurimo

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-AdhaEid al-Adha (yagereranijwe)

  • Wed, Jul 1
    Independence DayUmunsi w'Ubwigenge

  • Sat, Jul 4
    Liberation DayUmunsi wo Kwibohora

  • Mon, Jul 6
    Umunsi wo Kwibohora (yizihijwe)
  • Fri, Aug 7
    Umuganura DayUmunsi w'Umuganura

    Umuganura Day is a Rwandan public holiday observed on the first Friday of August. It is a traditional harvest festival honouring agricultural and food security, revived in 2011 after being suppressed during the colonial era.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryIjyanwa mu Ijuru rya Bikiramariya

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Mon, Aug 17

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNoheli

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Boxing DayUmunsi ukurikira Noheli

    Boxing Day is a holiday observed on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day. Originating in the United Kingdom, it is a public holiday in many Commonwealth countries and is traditionally a day for sport, family visits, and shopping.

    Umunsi ukurikira Noheli (yizihijwe)

🇭🇹 Haiti Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Haiti. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

18 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    National Independence DayFête de l'Indépendance Nationale

    National Independence Day is a national observance held in many countries to commemorate the date they gained independence from another nation or colonial power. The specific date varies by country.

  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNouvel An

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Ancestry DayJour des Aïeux

    Ancestry Day is a public holiday in Haiti on 2 January, the day after Independence Day. It honours the ancestors and the heroes of the Haitian Revolution who won the country's independence in 1804.

  • Sun, Feb 15
    CarnivalCarnaval

    Carnival is a festive season held immediately before Lent in Catholic and some Lutheran traditions. It features public celebrations, parades, masquerades, and feasting, and is observed in many countries in Europe and the Americas.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Shrove MondayLundi Gras

    Shrove Monday (Lundi Gras) is a public holiday in Haiti on the Monday before Ash Wednesday. It is one of the main days of the Haitian Carnival (Kanaval).

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Mardi Gras

    Fat Tuesday, or Mardi Gras, is a public holiday in Haiti on the last day of Carnival, the Tuesday before Ash Wednesday. It closes the Carnival festivities with parades and music before Lent begins.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayVendredi Saint

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPâques

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Fri, May 1
    Agriculture and Labour DayFête de l'Agriculture et du Travail

    Agriculture and Labor Day is a public holiday in Haiti observed on 1 May. It honours rural workers and the labour movement, combining the international Labour Day with recognition of the country's agricultural heritage.

  • Mon, May 18
    Flag and University DayFête du Drapeau et de l'Université

    Flag and University Day is a Haitian public holiday on 18 May. It commemorates the creation of the Haitian flag on 18 May 1803 at the Congress of Arcahaie, and since 1919 has also honoured the country's universities and students.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiFête-Dieu

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssomption de Marie

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Sat, Oct 17
    Death of DessalinesMort de Dessalines

    Death of Dessalines is a Haitian public holiday on 17 October. It marks the 1806 assassination of Jean-Jacques Dessalines, leader of the Haitian Revolution and first ruler of independent Haiti.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayLa Toussaint

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    Day of the DeadFête des Morts

    The Day of the Dead (Fête des Morts) is a public holiday in Haiti on 2 November. Families visit cemeteries to honour their ancestors, and the day is also associated with the Gede spirits of Vodou.

  • Wed, Nov 18
    Armed Forces DayJour des Forces Armées

    Armed Forces Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour the men and women who serve in the country's military. The specific date varies by national tradition.

  • Wed, Nov 18
    Battle of Vertières DayCommémoration de la Bataille de Vertières

    Haitian public holiday on 18 November marking the 1803 Battle of Vertières, the decisive victory securing Haitian independence from France.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNoël

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

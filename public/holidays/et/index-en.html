🇪🇹 Ethiopia Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Ethiopia. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Wed, Jan 7
    Christmas Dayየገና ወይም የልደት በዓል

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Mon, Jan 19
    Epiphanyየጥምቀት በዓል

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    Adwa Victory Dayየአድዋ ድል በዓል

    Adwa Victory Day is a national holiday in Ethiopia that commemorates Ethiopia's victory against Italian forces at the Battle of Adwa on 1 March 1896.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-Fitrየኢድ አልፈጥር (ግምት)

    Eid al-Fitr, the festival marking the end of the Ramadan fast, is a public holiday in Ethiopia. Its date follows the Islamic calendar and is estimated in advance.

  • Fri, Apr 10
    Good Fridayየስቅለት በዓል

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 12
    Easter Sundayየትንሳኤ(ፋሲካ) በዓል

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' Dayየዓለም የሠራተኞች (የላብአደሮች) ቀን

    International Workers' Day, also known as Labour Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working class held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Tue, May 5
    Patriots' Victory Dayየአርበኞች (የድል) ቀን በዓል

    Ethiopian Patriots' Victory Day, on 5 May, honours the patriots (Arbegnoch) who resisted the Italian occupation. It marks Emperor Haile Selassie's return to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941, five years after Italian troops entered the city.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-Adhaየኢድ አልአድሃ (አረፋ) (ግምት)

    Eid al-Adha, known in Ethiopia as Arefa, is the Islamic Feast of Sacrifice and a public holiday in Ethiopia. Its date follows the Islamic calendar and is estimated in advance.

  • Thu, May 28
    Downfall of the Derg Regime Dayደርግ የወደቀበት ቀን

    Downfall of the Dergue Regime Day is an Ethiopian public holiday observed on 28 May. It commemorates the 1991 fall of the Marxist-Leninist military government known as the Derg, ending the seventeen-year Ethiopian Civil War.

  • Tue, Aug 25
    Prophet Muhammad's Birthdayየመውሊድ በዓል (ግምት)

    Mawlid, the birthday of the Prophet Muhammad, is a public holiday in Ethiopia. It falls on 12 Rabi al-Awwal in the Islamic calendar, so the date is estimated in advance.

  • Fri, Sep 11
    Ethiopian New Yearየዘመን መለወጫ (እንቁጣጣሽ) በዓል

    Ethiopian New Year, called Enkutatash, falls on 11 September, or 12 September in the year before a leap year, and marks the start of the year in the Ethiopian calendar. It is a public holiday in Ethiopia and Eritrea.

  • Sun, Sep 27
    Finding of the True Cross (Meskel)የመስቀል በዓል

    The Finding of the True Cross, known as Meskel, is an Ethiopian public holiday on 27 September, or 28 September in some years. It recalls Saint Helena's finding of the cross and is celebrated with large bonfires called Demera.

🇧🇷 Brazil Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Brazil. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

10 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    Universal Fraternization DayConfraternização Universal

    Universal Fraternization Day (Confraternização Universal) is Brazil's New Year's Day holiday on 1 January, presented as a day of universal brotherhood and peace.

  • Sun, Jan 4
    State Creation DayCriação do EstadoRegional

    A state holiday in several Brazilian states marking their creation: Mato Grosso do Sul on 11 October, Rondônia on 4 January, and Roraima and Tocantins on 5 October, the date of the 1988 Constitution.

  • Fri, Jan 23
    Evangelical DayDia do EvangélicoRegional

    A holiday honoring Brazil's evangelical Christians in several states on dates set by each, such as late January in Acre, June 18 in Rondônia and November 30 in the Federal District.

  • Sun, Jan 25
    São Paulo City AnniversaryAniversário da Cidade de São PauloRegional

    A municipal holiday in the city of São Paulo, Brazil, on 25 January, marking the city's founding by Jesuit missionaries in 1554.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    CarnivalCarnavalRegional

    Carnival is a festive season held immediately before Lent in Catholic and some Lutheran traditions. It features public celebrations, parades, masquerades, and feasting, and is observed in many countries in Europe and the Americas.

  • Fri, Mar 6
    Pernambucan Revolution DayRevolução Pernambucana de 1817Regional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Pernambuco on 6 March, marking the start of the 1817 Pernambucan Revolution against Portuguese rule.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    International Women's DayDia Internacional da MulherRegional

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Wed, Mar 18
    Autonomy DayDia da AutonomiaRegional

    Held on 29 April on the island of Príncipe (São Tomé and Príncipe), autonomous since 1995, and on 18 March in the Brazilian state of Tocantins, recalling the 1809 creation of its northern district.

  • Thu, Mar 19
    Saint Joseph's DaySão JoséRegional

    Saint Joseph's Day is the Christian feast honouring Saint Joseph, the husband of Mary and earthly father of Jesus. It is observed on 19 March by Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some other Christian traditions.

  • Wed, Mar 25
    Abolition of Slavery in CearáAbolição da escravidão no CearáRegional

    A state holiday in the Brazilian state of Ceará on 25 March, marking 1884, when Ceará became the first Brazilian province to abolish slavery, four years before the rest of the country.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridaySexta-feira Santa

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 13
    Our Lady of PenhaNossa Senhora da PenhaRegional

    A state holiday in Espírito Santo, Brazil, honoring its patroness, held on the Monday eight days after Easter; it became a state holiday in 2019.

  • Tue, Apr 21
    Tiradentes' DayTiradentes

    Tiradentes' Day is a national public holiday in Brazil on 21 April honoring Joaquim José da Silva Xavier, known as Tiradentes, executed in 1792 as leader of the Inconfidência Mineira, an early movement for Brazilian independence.

  • Tue, Apr 21
    Founding of BrasíliaFundação de BrasíliaRegional

    A holiday in Brazil's Federal District on 21 April, marking the inauguration of Brasília as the national capital in 1960; the date also coincides with the national Tiradentes Day.

  • Tue, Apr 21
    Tiradentes' ExecutionExecução de TiradentesRegional

    A state holiday in Minas Gerais, Brazil, on April 21, the anniversary of the 1792 execution of Tiradentes, leader of the Minas conspiracy; the same date is also a national holiday.

  • Thu, Apr 23
    Saint George's DaySão JorgeRegional

    Saint George's Day honours the Christian martyr Saint George. It is a public holiday in Georgia on 23 November; on 23 April it is a holiday in Rio de Janeiro state, in Aragon in Spain, and in some Italian towns that have him as patron.

  • Fri, May 1
    Workers' DayDia do Trabalhador

    Worker's Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May, the International Workers' Day, while some nations observe their own date.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiRegional

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Mon, Jun 15
    Acre Statehood DayAniversário do AcreRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Acre on 15 June, marking the anniversary of Acre's elevation from federal territory to state in 1962.

  • Thu, Jun 18
    Evangelical DayDia do EvangélicoRegional

    A holiday honoring Brazil's evangelical Christians in several states on dates set by each, such as late January in Acre, June 18 in Rondônia and November 30 in the Federal District.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Saint John's DaySão JoãoRegional

    The feast of Saint John the Baptist, usually on 24 June, a local holiday in cities such as Porto and Braga in Portugal, Florence, Genoa and Turin in Italy, and in Alagoas, Brazil.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saint Peter's DaySão PedroRegional

    The feast of Saint Peter on 29 June, observed as a local holiday in parts of Andorra and Portugal and in the Brazilian state of Alagoas.

  • Thu, Jul 2
    Bahia Independence DayIndependência da BahiaRegional

    A state holiday in Bahia, Brazil, on 2 July, marking the expulsion of Portuguese troops from Salvador in 1823, which sealed Brazilian independence in the region.

  • Wed, Jul 8
    Sergipe Emancipation DayEmancipação política de SergipeRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Sergipe on 8 July, marking its political emancipation from Bahia in 1820.

  • Thu, Jul 9
    Constitutionalist Revolution DayRevolução ConstitucionalistaRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of São Paulo on 9 July, marking the start of the 1932 Constitutionalist Revolution against the government of Getúlio Vargas.

  • Sat, Jul 25
    Saint James' DaySão TiagoRegional

    The feast of Saint James the Apostle on 25 July, a regional holiday in parts of Spain, in the Argentine province of Mendoza and in places in Brazil.

  • Sun, Jul 26
    Foundation of the City of GoiásFundação da cidade de GoiásRegional

    A state holiday in Goiás, Brazil, on July 26, marking the founding of the city of Goiás, the former state capital, by Bartolomeu Bueno da Silva in 1727.

  • Tue, Jul 28
    Maranhão's Adhesion to Brazilian IndependenceAdesão do Maranhão à independência do BrasilRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Maranhão on 28 July, marking the province's adhesion to Brazil's independence in 1823.

  • Wed, Aug 5
    Paraíba State Foundation DayFundação do EstadoRegional

    A state holiday in Paraíba, Brazil, on 5 August, marking the founding of the state's capital, João Pessoa, in 1585.

  • Fri, Aug 7
    Rio Grande do Norte DayDia do Rio Grande do NorteRegional

    The state day of Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil, on 7 August. It recalls the arrival of Portuguese ships in 1501 and the Marco de Touros, an early stone marker of Portuguese presence in Brazil.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Adhesion of Grão-Pará to the Independence of BrazilAdesão do Grão-Pará à independência do BrasilRegional

    A state holiday in Pará, Brazil, on August 15, marking the day in 1823 when the province of Grão-Pará joined the independent Brazilian Empire.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Our Lady of the AssumptionNossa Senhora da AssunçãoRegional

    The feast of the Assumption of Mary on 15 August, listed as a holiday in the Brazilian state of Ceará; Our Lady of the Assumption is patron of its capital, Fortaleza.

  • Sun, Aug 16
    Santa Catarina State DayDia do Estado de Santa CatarinaRegional

    The state day of Santa Catarina, Brazil, marking the creation of the Captaincy of Santa Catarina on 11 August 1738. Since 2022 it is observed on the Sunday after 11 August.

  • Sat, Sep 5
    Amazon DayDia da AmazôniaRegional

    A state holiday in the Brazilian state of Acre honouring the Amazon on 5 September, the date the province of Amazonas was created in 1850. Acre moves it to a Friday when it falls on a weekday.

  • Sat, Sep 5
    Elevation of Amazonas to ProvinceElevação do Amazonas à categoria de provínciaRegional

    A state holiday in the Brazilian state of Amazonas on 5 September, marking the law of 1850 that raised Amazonas from a district of Grão-Pará to a province of the Empire of Brazil.

  • Mon, Sep 7
    Independence DayIndependência do Brasil

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Tue, Sep 8
    Our Lady of the NativityNossa Senhora da NatividadeRegional

    A state holiday in Tocantins, Brazil, on 8 September, the feast of the Nativity of Mary; Our Lady of the Nativity is the state's patron saint.

  • Sun, Sep 13
    Creation of the Federal Territory of AmapáCriação do Território FederalRegional

    A state holiday in the Brazilian state of Amapá on 13 September, marking the creation of the Federal Territory of Amapá in 1943; it became a state in 1988.

  • Wed, Sep 16
    Political Emancipation of AlagoasEmancipação Política de AlagoasRegional

    A state holiday in Alagoas, Brazil, on September 16, marking its separation from Pernambuco as an independent captaincy in 1817.

  • Sun, Sep 20
    Gaucho DayDia do GaúchoRegional

    A public holiday in the Brazilian state of Rio Grande do Sul on 20 September, marking the start of the Ragamuffin (Farroupilha) Revolution in 1835.

  • Sat, Oct 3
    Martyrs of Cunhaú and Uruaçu DayMártires de Cunhaú e UruaçuRegional

    A state holiday in Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil, on 3 October, honouring the Catholic martyrs of the 1645 Cunhaú and Uruaçu massacres, canonised in 2017.

  • Mon, Oct 5
    State Creation DayCriação do EstadoRegional

    A state holiday in several Brazilian states marking their creation: Mato Grosso do Sul on 11 October, Rondônia on 4 January, and Roraima and Tocantins on 5 October, the date of the 1988 Constitution.

  • Sun, Oct 11
    State Creation DayCriação do EstadoRegional

    A state holiday in several Brazilian states marking their creation: Mato Grosso do Sul on 11 October, Rondônia on 4 January, and Roraima and Tocantins on 5 October, the date of the 1988 Constitution.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Our Lady of AparecidaNossa Senhora Aparecida

    Our Lady of Aparecida is a Brazilian public holiday on 12 October honouring the country's patroness. It recalls a small image of the Virgin Mary that fishermen found in the Paraíba River in 1717.

  • Mon, Oct 19
    Piauí DayDia do PiauíRegional

    A state holiday in the Brazilian state of Piauí on 19 October, commemorating the declaration of support for Brazilian independence made in the town of Parnaíba.

  • Sat, Oct 24
    Foundation of GoiâniaPedra fundamental de GoiâniaRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Goiás on 24 October, marking the laying of the cornerstone of Goiânia, the planned state capital, on 24 October 1933.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' DayFinados

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Sun, Nov 15
    Republic Proclamation DayProclamação da República

    Republic Proclamation Day is a Brazilian public holiday on 15 November. It commemorates the proclamation of the republic in 1889, which ended the Empire of Brazil.

  • Sun, Nov 15
    Our Lady of RocioNossa Senhora do RocioRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Paraná on 15 November honouring Nossa Senhora do Rocio, the patroness of the state, whose shrine is in Paranaguá.

  • Fri, Nov 20
    Zumbi and Black Awareness DayDia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra

    Brazilian national holiday on 20 November honouring Zumbi dos Palmares, leader of the Quilombo dos Palmares, and the Black community of Brazil.

  • Fri, Nov 20
    Treaty of Petrópolis DayAssinatura do Tratado de PetrópolisRegional

    A state holiday in Acre, Brazil, around 17 November, marking the 1903 Treaty of Petrópolis, by which Bolivia ceded the Acre territory to Brazil.

  • Sun, Nov 29
    Saint Catherine of Alexandria DayDia de Santa Catarina de AlexandriaRegional

    A holiday in the Brazilian state of Santa Catarina honouring its namesake patron, Saint Catherine of Alexandria, whose feast is 25 November; it is marked on the following Sunday.

  • Mon, Nov 30
    Evangelical DayDia do EvangélicoRegional

    A holiday honoring Brazil's evangelical Christians in several states on dates set by each, such as late January in Acre, June 18 in Rondônia and November 30 in the Federal District.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNatal

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

🇲🇿 Mozambique Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Mozambique. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

10 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    Universal Fraternity DayDia da Fraternidade universal

    Universal Fraternity Day is observed in Mozambique on 1 January, coinciding with New Year's Day. It is a public holiday dedicated to brotherhood among all people.

  • Tue, Feb 3
    Heroes' DayDia dos Heróis Moçambicanos

    Heroes' Day honours those who fought for independence and freedom. Mozambique marks it on 3 February, Zambia on the first Monday of July, and Namibia on 26 August.

  • Tue, Apr 7
    Women's DayDia da Mulher Moçambicana

    Women's Day is an observance celebrating the achievements of women and advocating gender equality. In many countries it coincides with International Women's Day on 8 March; some nations mark their own national women's day.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' DayDia Internacional dos Trabalhadores

    International Workers' Day, also known as Labour Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working class held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Thu, Jun 25
    Independence DayDia da Independência Nacional

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Mon, Sep 7
    Victory DayDia da Vitória

    Victory Day commemorates the end of a war or a decisive military victory. The specific date and conflict marked differ by country, with notable examples including the end of World War II in Europe celebrated on 8 or 9 May.

  • Fri, Sep 25
    Armed Forces DayDia das Forças Armadas de Libertação Nacional

    Armed Forces Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour the men and women who serve in the country's military. The specific date varies by national tradition.

  • Sun, Oct 4
    Peace and Reconciliation DayDia da Paz e Reconciliação

    Peace and Reconciliation Day is a Mozambican public holiday observed on 4 October. It commemorates the 1992 Rome General Peace Accords that ended the 16-year Mozambican Civil War.

  • Mon, Oct 5
    Dia da Paz e Reconciliação (ponte)
  • Fri, Dec 25
    Family DayDia da Família

    Family Day is a public holiday observed in several countries to celebrate the importance of family. The specific date varies, with Canadian provinces and South Africa among the better-known observers.

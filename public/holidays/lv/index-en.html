🇱🇻 Latvia Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Latvia. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

18 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayJaungada diena

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Transferred day offBrīvdiena (pārcelta no 17.01.2026)

    A day off in Latvia created by moving a working day: this weekday before or after a holiday is free, and the lost working time is made up on Saturday 17 January 2026.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayLielā Piektdiena

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPirmās Lieldienas

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayOtrās Lieldienas

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Constitutional Assembly Convocation DayLatvijas Republikas Satversmes sapulces sasaukšanas diena

    Constitutional Assembly Convocation Day (Satversmes sapulces sasaukšanas diena) is a Latvian remembrance day on 1 May for the 1920 convening of the first elected Constitutional Assembly, which drafted the 1922 Satversme.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayDarba svētki

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Mon, May 4
    Restoration of Independence DayLatvijas Republikas Neatkarības atjaunošanas diena

    Restoration of Independence Day is a national holiday in Latvia (4 May, marking 1990), Portugal (1 December, marking 1640) and Timor-Leste (20 May, marking the restoration of independence in 2002).

  • Sun, May 10
    Mother's DayMātes diena

    Mother's Day is a celebration honouring mothers, motherhood, and the influence of mothers in society. In most countries it falls on the second Sunday of May, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Sun, May 24
    PentecostVasarsvētki

    Whit Sunday, also called Pentecost, is the Christian feast celebrating the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles fifty days after Easter. It is observed on the seventh Sunday after Easter Sunday.

  • Mon, Jun 22
    Transferred day offBrīvdiena (pārcelta no 27.06.2026)

    A day off in Latvia created by moving a working day: this weekday before or after a holiday is free, and the lost working time is made up on Saturday 27 June 2026.

  • Tue, Jun 23
    Midsummer EveLīgo diena

    Midsummer Eve is the evening before Midsummer Day, celebrating the summer solstice. It is a public holiday in Finland (a Friday in late June) and Latvia (Līgo Day, 23 June), marked by bonfires and festivities.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Midsummer DayJāņu diena

    Midsummer Day is a celebration of the summer solstice and the longest days of the year, observed on or around 24 June in Northern Europe. In Christian tradition it is also associated with the feast of John the Baptist.

  • Wed, Nov 18
    Republic of Latvia Proclamation DayLatvijas Republikas Proklamēšanas diena

    Republic of Latvia Proclamation Day is observed on 18 November. It commemorates the 1918 proclamation of the Republic of Latvia by the People's Council, declaring independence after the dissolution of the Russian Empire.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveZiemassvētku vakars

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayPirmie Ziemassvētki

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of ChristmasOtrie Ziemassvētki

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    New Year's EveVecgada diena

    New Year's Eve is the evening or last day of the year in the Gregorian calendar, observed on 31 December. It is marked worldwide with social gatherings, fireworks, and traditions that lead into the New Year.

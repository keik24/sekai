🇦🇩 Andorra Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Andorra. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

14 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayCap d'Any

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyReis

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Wed, Jan 7
    Saint Julian's DaySant JuliàRegional

    A holiday in the Andorran parish of Sant Julià de Lòria on 7 January, the feast of the parish's patron saint.

  • Sat, Jan 17
    Saint Anthony's DaySant AntoniRegional

    Saint Anthony's Day is a local holiday in parts of Andorra, Spain and Portugal. Depending on the place it honours Anthony the Abbot on 17 January or Anthony of Padua on 13 June, notably in Lisbon.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    CarnivalCarnaval

    Carnival is a festive season held immediately before Lent in Catholic and some Lutheran traditions. It features public celebrations, parades, masquerades, and feasting, and is observed in many countries in Europe and the Americas.

  • Sat, Mar 14
    Constitution DayDia de la Constitució

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayDivendres Sant

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayDilluns de Pasqua

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayFesta del treball

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Thu, May 7
    Saint Michael of EngolastersSant Miquel d'EngolastersRegional

    A local holiday in the Andorran parish of Escaldes-Engordany on 7 May, a feast of Saint Michael associated with the Romanesque church of Sant Miquel d'Engolasters.

  • Mon, May 25
    Whit MondayDilluns de Pentecosta

    Whit Monday, also called Pentecost Monday, is the Christian holiday on the day after Pentecost, the seventh Sunday after Easter. It is a public holiday in many countries in Europe, Africa and the Caribbean.

  • Sat, May 30
    Our Lady of CanòlichDiada de CanòlichRegional

    A local holiday in the Andorran parish of Sant Julià de Lòria on the last Saturday of May, when people make a pilgrimage to the Sanctuary of Canòlich, dedicated to the parish's patroness.

  • Sun, Jun 14
    Escaldes-Engordany Parish DayDiada de la creació de la parròquiaRegional

    A local holiday in the Andorran parish of Escaldes-Engordany, marking its creation as Andorra's seventh parish on 14 June 1978. It is held on a Sunday in mid-June.

  • Mon, Jun 29
    Saint Peter's DaySant PereRegional

    The feast of Saint Peter on 29 June, observed as a local holiday in parts of Andorra and Portugal and in the Brazilian state of Alagoas.

  • Sat, Jul 25
    Escaldes-Engordany Festa MajorFesta Major d'Escaldes-EngordanyRegional

    The annual festival (Festa Major) of the Andorran parish of Escaldes-Engordany, with parish holidays on 25 and 26 July, the days of its patrons Saint James and Saint Anne.

  • Sun, Jul 26
    Escaldes-Engordany Festa MajorFesta Major d'Escaldes-EngordanyRegional

    The annual festival (Festa Major) of the Andorran parish of Escaldes-Engordany, with parish holidays on 25 and 26 July, the days of its patrons Saint James and Saint Anne.

  • Mon, Jul 27
    Sant Julià de Lòria FestivalFesta Major de Sant Julià de LòriaRegional

    Local holidays in the parish of Sant Julià de Lòria, Andorra, for its annual Festa Major, held at the end of July.

  • Tue, Jul 28
    Sant Julià de Lòria FestivalFesta Major de Sant Julià de LòriaRegional

    Local holidays in the parish of Sant Julià de Lòria, Andorra, for its annual Festa Major, held at the end of July.

  • Sat, Aug 1
    Andorra la Vella FestivalFesta Major d'Andorra la VellaRegional

    The Festa Major of Andorra la Vella, the annual festival of Andorra's capital parish, held over three days from the first Saturday of August and observed as a local holiday in the parish.

  • Sun, Aug 2
    Andorra la Vella FestivalFesta Major d'Andorra la VellaRegional

    The Festa Major of Andorra la Vella, the annual festival of Andorra's capital parish, held over three days from the first Saturday of August and observed as a local holiday in the parish.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    Andorra la Vella FestivalFesta Major d'Andorra la VellaRegional

    The Festa Major of Andorra la Vella, the annual festival of Andorra's capital parish, held over three days from the first Saturday of August and observed as a local holiday in the parish.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssumpció

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Sun, Aug 16
    Saint Roch's DaySant RocRegional

    The feast of Saint Roch on 16 August, observed as a local holiday in the Andorran parish of Canillo.

  • Tue, Sep 8
    Our Lady of MeritxellNostra Senyora de Meritxell

    Our Lady of Meritxell is the patroness of Andorra, venerated through a Marian statue housed at the Sanctuary of Meritxell. The feast day on 8 September coincides with Andorra's National Day.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayTots Sants

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionImmaculada Concepció

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNadal

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DaySant Esteve

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

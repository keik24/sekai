🇬🇫 French Guiana Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in French Guiana. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

12 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayJour de l'an

    New Year's Day, on 1 January, marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayLundi de Pâques

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, extending the Easter celebrations. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayFête du Travail

    Labour Day, held on 1 May, honours workers and the labour movement as part of International Workers' Day. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Fri, May 8
    Victory in Europe DayFête de la Victoire

    Fête de la Victoire is the French observance held on 8 May to commemorate the Allied victory in Europe and the end of World War II in 1945. It is a public holiday in France.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayAscension

    Ascension Day is a Christian feast commemorating the ascension of Jesus into heaven, held on a Thursday forty days after Easter. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Mon, May 25
    Whit MondayLundi de Pentecôte

    Whit Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Pentecost Sunday, the seventh Sunday after Easter. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Wed, Jun 10
    Abolition of SlaveryAbolition de l'esclavage

    Abolition de l'esclavage commemorates the abolition of slavery in the French overseas departments and territories. Each territory observes it on its own date, reflecting its local history of emancipation.

  • Tue, Jul 14
    Bastille DayFête nationale

    Bastille Day, the French national day on 14 July, commemorates the storming of the Bastille in 1789 and the start of the French Revolution. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssomption

    The Assumption of Mary is a Catholic feast held on 15 August, commemorating the belief that Mary was taken bodily into heaven. It is a public holiday in France, its overseas territories and Burundi.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayToussaint

    Toussaint, the French name for All Saints' Day, is a Christian feast honouring all the saints on 1 November. It is a public holiday in Burundi and in France's overseas departments and territories.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    Armistice DayArmistice

    Armistice Day, observed on 11 November, marks the end of the fighting of the First World War in 1918 and honours those who died in the war. It is a public holiday in France and its overseas territories.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNoël

    Christmas Day is the Christian feast of the birth of Jesus, observed on 25 December. It is a public holiday throughout France's overseas departments and territories.

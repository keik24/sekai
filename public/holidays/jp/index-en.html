🇯🇵 Japan Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Japan. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

18 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day元日

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Jan 12
    Coming of Age Day成人の日

    Coming of Age Day is a public holiday in Japan held annually on the second Monday of January under the Happy Monday System.

  • Wed, Feb 11
    Foundation Day建国記念の日

    Foundation Day marks the founding of a nation or settlement. Japan observes National Foundation Day on 11 February, and Norfolk Island observes it on 6 March, the anniversary of the first British settlement in 1788.

  • Mon, Feb 23
    Emperor's Birthday天皇誕生日

    The Emperor's Birthday is a Japanese public holiday celebrating the birthday of the reigning emperor. Since 2020 the date is 23 February, the birthday of Emperor Naruhito, who acceded to the Chrysanthemum Throne in 2019.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Vernal Equinox Day春分の日

    Vernal Equinox Day is a Japanese public holiday observed on 20 or 21 March, the day of the spring equinox. It is a day to admire nature and cherish living things, and falls in Higan week, when families visit ancestral graves.

  • Wed, Apr 29
    Showa Day昭和の日

    Showa Day is a Japanese public holiday observed on 29 April, the birthday of Emperor Shōwa (Hirohito, 1901–1989). It encourages reflection on the Shōwa era and starts the Golden Week, a series of public holidays in late April and early May.

  • Sun, May 3
    Constitution Day憲法記念日

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Mon, May 4
    Greenery Dayみどりの日

    Greenery Day is a Japanese public holiday on 4 May, part of Golden Week. It is a day to enjoy nature and be grateful for its blessings.

  • Tue, May 5
    Children's Dayこどもの日

    Children's Day is an observance celebrating children and promoting their welfare. The United Nations marks it on 20 November, but many countries hold their own Children's Day on varying dates.

  • Wed, May 6
    Substitute Holiday振替休日

    Substitute Holiday (furikae kyūjitsu) is a day off in Japan given when a national holiday falls on a Sunday. The next weekday that is not itself a holiday becomes a day off in its place.

  • Mon, Jul 20
    Marine Day海の日

    Marine Day, also known as Ocean Day or Sea Day, is a national holiday in Japan on the third Monday of July. It is a day to give thanks for the blessings of the ocean.

  • Tue, Aug 11
    Mountain Day山の日

    Mountain Day is a national holiday in Japan on 11 August, a day to become familiar with mountains and appreciate their blessings. It was first observed in 2016.

  • Mon, Sep 21
    Respect for the Aged Day敬老の日

    Respect for the Aged Day is a Japanese public holiday observed on the third Monday of September. Established in 1966, it honours elderly citizens and is marked by community gatherings and ceremonies recognising long-lived residents.

  • Tue, Sep 22
    National Holiday国民の休日

    A public holiday observed nationwide; what it marks differs from country to country. In Japan, for example, a weekday that falls between two national holidays becomes a day off.

  • Wed, Sep 23
    Autumnal Equinox Day秋分の日

    Autumnal Equinox Day is a public holiday in Japan that usually occurs on September 22 or 23, the date of Southward equinox in Japan Standard Time.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Sports Dayスポーツの日

    Sports Day is a Japanese public holiday observed on the second Monday of October. Established in 1966 to commemorate the 1964 Tokyo Olympics, it promotes physical fitness and is marked by sports events at schools and communities nationwide.

  • Tue, Nov 3
    Culture Day文化の日

    Culture Day is a public holiday in Japan held annually on November 3 to promote culture, the arts, and academic endeavour.

  • Mon, Nov 23
    Labor Thanksgiving Day勤労感謝の日

    Labor Thanksgiving Day is a national holiday in Japan on 23 November, a day to honour labour and production and for people to thank one another. When it falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is a substitute holiday.

  • Wed, Jan 7
    Saint John the Baptist's DaySoborul Sfântului Proroc Ioan Botezătorul

  • Sat, Jan 24
    Unification of the Romanian Principalities DayZiua Unirii Principatelor Române

    Unification of the Romanian Principalities Day is a Romanian public holiday observed on 24 January. It commemorates the 1859 union of Wallachia and Moldavia under Alexandru Ioan Cuza, the foundation of the modern Romanian state.

    Dormition of the Mother of GodAdormirea Maicii Domnului

  • Mon, Nov 30
    Saint Andrew's DaySfântul Apostol Andrei, cel Întâi chemat, Ocrotitorul României

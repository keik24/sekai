🇻🇪 Venezuela Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Venezuela. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

15 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAño Nuevo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Carnival MondayLunes de Carnaval

    Carnival Monday is a public holiday during the carnival season. In some countries it falls before Ash Wednesday, while several Caribbean islands hold their carnival and this holiday in summer.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Carnival TuesdayMartes de Carnaval

    Carnival Tuesday is the last day of carnival and a public holiday in several countries. Where carnival precedes Lent it is Shrove Tuesday, the day before Ash Wednesday, while some Caribbean islands hold it in summer.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Maundy ThursdayJueves Santo

    Maundy Thursday, also called Holy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayViernes Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sun, Apr 19
    Declaration of IndependenceDeclaración de la Independencia

    Declaration of Independence is a Venezuelan public holiday on 19 April. It marks the events of 19 April 1810 in Caracas, when the Spanish governor was deposed and the country's path to independence began.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' DayDia Mundial del Trabajador

    International Worker's Day, also known as Labour Day or May Day, is a celebration of workers and the labour movement held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago.

  • Fri, May 15
    Family DayDía de la Familia

    Family Day is a public holiday observed in several countries to celebrate the importance of family. The specific date varies, with Canadian provinces and South Africa among the better-known observers.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Battle of CaraboboBatalla de Carabobo

    The Battle of Carabobo is a public holiday in Venezuela on 24 June. It commemorates the 1821 victory of Simón Bolívar's independence forces over the Spanish royalists, which decided Venezuela's independence.

  • Sun, Jul 5
    Independence DayDía de la Independencia

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Jul 24
    Birthday of Simón BolívarNatalicio de Simón Bolívar

    The Birthday of Simón Bolívar is a public holiday in Venezuela on 24 July. It honours Bolívar, born in 1783, the Liberator who led several South American countries to independence from Spain.

  • Mon, Oct 12
    Day of Indigenous ResistanceDía de la Resistencia Indígena

    The Day of Indigenous Resistance is a public holiday in Venezuela on 12 October. Renamed from Columbus Day in 2002, it honours the resistance of indigenous peoples to European conquest.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveNochebuena

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDía de Navidad

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    New Year's EveFiesta de Fin de Año

    New Year's Eve is the evening or last day of the year in the Gregorian calendar, observed on 31 December. It is marked worldwide with social gatherings, fireworks, and traditions that lead into the New Year.

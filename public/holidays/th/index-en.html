🇹🇭 Thailand Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Thailand. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

28 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Dayวันขึ้นปีใหม่

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2
    Bridge Holidayวันหยุดพิเศษ (เพิ่มเติม)

    An extra non-working day that links a public holiday to a weekend to create a long break; Argentina sets such days for tourism and Thailand declares special holidays for the same purpose.

  • Sat, Jan 10
    National Children's Dayวันเด็กแห่งชาติ

    National Children's Day celebrates children and their welfare. In Thailand it falls on the second Saturday of January, and in Tuvalu, where it is called Aso Tamaliki, on the first Monday of August.

  • Tue, Mar 3
    Makha Buchaวันมาฆบูชา

    Makha Bucha is a Buddhist festival observed on the full moon of the third lunar month. It commemorates the spontaneous gathering of 1,250 monks before the Buddha and his delivery of the Ovadapatimokkha. It is a public holiday in Thailand.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Chakri Memorial Dayวันพระบาทสมเด็จพระพุทธยอดฟ้าจุฬาโลกมหาราช และวันที่ระลึกมหาจักรีบรมราชวงศ์

    Thai public holiday on 6 April commemorating the founding of the Chakri Dynasty in 1782 by King Rama I.

  • Mon, Apr 13
    Songkranวันสงกรานต์

    Songkran is a Thai public holiday on 13-15 April marking the solar new year. Known for water-splashing as a symbol of cleansing and renewal, plus family gatherings. UNESCO inscribed it as Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2023.

  • Tue, Apr 14
    Songkranวันสงกรานต์

    Songkran is a Thai public holiday on 13-15 April marking the solar new year. Known for water-splashing as a symbol of cleansing and renewal, plus family gatherings. UNESCO inscribed it as Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2023.

  • Wed, Apr 15
    Songkranวันสงกรานต์

    National Labour Day is a public holiday in Thailand on 1 May, honouring workers. It is mainly a day off for the private sector.

  • Mon, May 4
    Coronation Dayวันฉัตรมงคล

    Coronation Day (วันฉัตรมงคล, Wan Chatra Mongkhon) is a Thai public holiday on 4 May commemorating the 2019 coronation of King Maha Vajiralongkorn (Rama X), who succeeded his father Bhumibol Adulyadej in 2016.

  • Sun, May 31
    Visakha Buchaวันวิสาขบูชา

    Visakha Bucha is a Buddhist public holiday in Thailand commemorating the birth, enlightenment and passing of the Buddha. It falls on the full moon of the sixth lunar month, usually in May or June.

  • Tue, Jul 28
    King Vajiralongkorn's Birthdayวันเฉลิมพระชนมพรรษาพระบาทสมเด็จพระปรเมนทรรามาธิบดีศรีสินทรมหาวชิราลงกรณ พระวชิรเกล้าเจ้าอยู่หัว

  • Thu, Jul 30
    Buddhist Lent Dayวันเข้าพรรษา

    Buddhist Lent Day (Khao Phansa) is a public holiday in Thailand on the day after Asarnha Bucha. It marks the start of the three-month rains retreat, when monks stay in their temples, and candles are offered at temples.

  • Wed, Aug 12

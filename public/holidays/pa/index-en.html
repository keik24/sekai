🇵🇦 Panama Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Panama. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

13 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAño Nuevo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 9
    Martyrs' DayDía de los Mártires

    Martyrs' Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour individuals who died for a national, religious, or political cause. The specific date and history vary by country.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Carnival TuesdayMartes de Carnaval

    Carnival Tuesday is the last day of carnival and a public holiday in several countries. Where carnival precedes Lent it is Shrove Tuesday, the day before Ash Wednesday, while some Caribbean islands hold it in summer.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayViernes Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayDía del Trabajo

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Tue, Nov 3
    Separation DaySeparación de Panamá de Colombia

    Separation Day in Panama, observed on 3 November, commemorates the 1903 separation of Panama from Colombia and the proclamation of the Republic of Panama. Known as Separacion de Panama de Colombia, it is a principal patriotic holiday.

  • Thu, Nov 5
    Colón DayDía de Colón

    Colon Day is a public holiday in Panama on 5 November. It commemorates the role of the city and province of Colón in Panama's separation from Colombia in 1903.

  • Tue, Nov 10
    Los Santos Uprising DayPrimer Grito de Independencia

    Los Santos Uprising Day is a Panamanian public holiday observed on 10 November. It commemorates the 1821 uprising in the town of Villa de Los Santos against Spanish rule, which precipitated Panama's separation from Spain.

  • Sat, Nov 28
    Independence DayIndependencia de Panamá de España

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Mother's DayDía de la Madre

    Mother's Day is a celebration honouring mothers, motherhood, and the influence of mothers in society. In most countries it falls on the second Sunday of May, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Sun, Dec 20
    National Mourning DayDía de Duelo Nacional

    National Mourning Day is a national observance held in several countries to honour the victims of a major tragedy or to mourn a significant national loss. The specific date and historical context vary by country.

  • Mon, Dec 21
    Día de Duelo Nacional (puente)
  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNavidad

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

🇲🇭 Marshall Islands Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Marshall Islands. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

11 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Sun, Mar 1
    Nuclear Victims Remembrance Day

    Nuclear Victims Remembrance Day is a national holiday in the Marshall Islands on 1 March. It marks the 1954 Castle Bravo test at Bikini Atoll and remembers those affected by nuclear testing.

  • Mon, Mar 2
    Nuclear Victims Remembrance Day (substitute holiday)Nuclear Victims Remembrance Day Holiday

    A day off in the Marshall Islands given when Nuclear Victims Remembrance Day (1 March), which honours the victims of US nuclear testing, falls on a weekend.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Constitution Day

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Fri, Jul 3
    Fisherman's Day

    Fisherman's Day is a public holiday in the Marshall Islands on the first Friday of July. It honours the fishers who support the islands' food supply, and is often marked with fishing tournaments.

  • Fri, Sep 4
    Dri-jerbal Day

    Dri-jerbal Day is the Labour Day of the Marshall Islands, observed on the first Friday of September. The name means workers in Marshallese, and the day honours the country's working people.

  • Fri, Sep 25
    Manit Day

    Manit Day is a public holiday in the Marshall Islands on the last Friday of September. It celebrates Marshallese culture and traditions; manit means custom or way of life in Marshallese.

  • Tue, Nov 17
    President's Day

    A holiday honouring a president or the office of the presidency. The occasion and date differ from country to country, such as a president's birthday or a day set by national law.

  • Fri, Dec 4
    Gospel Day

    Gospel Day is an official observance in several Pacific island nations that commemorates the arrival of the first Christian missionaries and the introduction of the Gospel to the islands.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

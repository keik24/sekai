🇦🇽 Åland Islands Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Åland Islands. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

16 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayUudenvuodenpäivä

    New Year's Day on 1 January marks the first day of the year in the Gregorian calendar. It is a public holiday in Åland.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyLoppiainen

    Loppiainen, Epiphany, is a public holiday in Åland on 6 January. It commemorates the visit of the Magi to the infant Jesus.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayPitkäperjantai

    Good Friday is the Christian commemoration of the crucifixion of Jesus, held on the Friday before Easter Sunday. It is a public holiday in Finland and the Åland Islands.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPääsiäispäivä

    Easter Sunday is the Christian feast of Jesus's resurrection. A movable holiday set by the Western computus, it is a public holiday in Åland, where most people are Lutheran.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayToinen pääsiäispäivä

    Easter Monday is the day after Easter Sunday, extending the Christian celebration of the resurrection of Jesus. It is a public holiday in Åland.

  • Fri, May 1
    May DayVappu

    Vappu is May Day, a public holiday on 1 May in the Åland Islands, as in the rest of Finland. It combines International Workers' Day with a spring festival, often celebrated with picnics and student festivities.

  • Thu, May 14
    Ascension DayHelatorstai

    Helatorstai, Ascension Day, is a public holiday in the Åland Islands, as in the rest of Finland. It falls on a Thursday 39 days after Easter Sunday and marks the ascension of Jesus into heaven.

  • Sun, May 24
    PentecostHelluntaipäivä

    Helluntaipäivä, Pentecost, is a public holiday in the Åland Islands, as in the rest of Finland. It falls on the seventh Sunday after Easter and marks the descent of the Holy Spirit upon the apostles.

  • Tue, Jun 9
    Åland Self-Government DayAhvenanmaan itsehallintopäivä

    Ahvenanmaan itsehallintopäivä is the Self-Government Day of the Åland Islands, observed on 9 June. It commemorates the first session of the Åland parliament in 1922, after the League of Nations recognised the islands' autonomy.

  • Fri, Jun 19
    Midsummer EveJuhannusaatto

    Juhannusaatto, Midsummer Eve, is a holiday in Åland on the Friday between 19 and 25 June. It is traditionally celebrated outdoors, with a maypole in Åland and Finland-style summer gatherings.

  • Sat, Jun 20
    Midsummer DayJuhannuspäivä

    Juhannuspäivä, Midsummer Day, is a public holiday in Åland on the Saturday between 20 and 26 June. It marks the midsummer season with outdoor festivities.

  • Sat, Oct 31
    All Saints' DayPyhäinpäivä

    All Saints' Day honours known and unknown saints and remembers the deceased. In Åland it is observed on the Saturday between 31 October and 6 November, following the Lutheran calendar.

  • Sun, Dec 6
    Independence DayItsenäisyyspäivä

    Itsenäisyyspäivä, Finland's Independence Day, is a public holiday in Åland on 6 December. It commemorates Finland's 1917 declaration of independence from the Russian Empire.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveJouluaatto

    Jouluaatto, Christmas Eve, is a holiday in Åland on 24 December. Families gather for the main Christmas meal, following the Finnish and Nordic tradition of celebrating mainly on the eve.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayJoulupäivä

    Joulupäivä, Christmas Day, is a public holiday in Åland on 25 December. It is the Christian celebration of the birth of Jesus, marked by church services and family gatherings.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DayTapaninpäivä

    Saint Stephen's Day on 26 December honours Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is a public holiday in Åland.

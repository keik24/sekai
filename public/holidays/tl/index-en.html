🇹🇱 Timor-Leste Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Timor-Leste. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

18 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayDia de Ano Novo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Mar 3
    Veterans' DayDia dos Veteranos

    Veterans' Day is a public holiday in Timor-Leste on 3 March. It honours the veterans who took part in the country's long struggle for independence.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-FitrIdul Fitri (aproximada)

    This is the estimated date of Idul Fitri, the festival marking the end of Ramadan, observed as a public holiday in Timor-Leste. The actual date depends on the Islamic lunar calendar.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridaySexta-Feira Santa

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' DayDia Mundial do Trabalhador

    International Worker's Day, also known as Labour Day or May Day, is a celebration of workers and the labour movement held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago.

  • Wed, May 20
    Restoration of Independence DayDia da Restauração da Independência

    Restoration of Independence Day is a national holiday in Latvia (4 May, marking 1990), Portugal (1 December, marking 1640) and Timor-Leste (20 May, marking the restoration of independence in 2002).

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-AdhaIdul Adha (aproximada)

    This is the estimated date of Idul Adha, the Islamic Feast of Sacrifice, observed as a public holiday in Timor-Leste. The feast falls on 10 Dhu al-Hijjah, so the actual date depends on the lunar calendar.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiFesta do Corpo de Deus

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Sun, Aug 30
    Popular Consultation DayDia da Consulta Popular

    Popular Consultation Day is a Timorese public holiday observed on 30 August. It commemorates the 1999 UN-supervised referendum in which the East Timorese voted for independence from Indonesia.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayDia de Todos os Santos

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' DayDia de Todos os Fiéis Defuntos

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Tue, Nov 3
    National Women's DayDia Nacional da Mulher

    National Women's Day celebrates women and their contributions. It is a public holiday in South Africa on 9 August, recalling the 1956 women's march, and in Timor-Leste on 3 November.

  • Thu, Nov 12
    National Youth DayDia Nacional da Juventude

    National Youth Day is a national observance held in many countries to recognise the contributions of young people. The specific date and origin vary by country, often tied to a historic youth movement.

  • Sat, Nov 28
    Proclamation of Independence DayDia da Proclamação da Independência

    Proclamation of Independence Day recalls Morocco's 1944 Independence Manifesto (11 January), Timor-Leste's 1975 declaration of independence (28 November) and Burkina Faso's 1958 proclamation of the Republic (11 December).

  • Mon, Dec 7
    Memorial DayDia da Memória

    Memorial Day is a day of remembrance for those who died in service to their country. In the United States it falls on the last Monday of May; other countries hold similar days on other dates, some also honouring disaster victims.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception, Patroness of Timor-LesteDia de Nossa Senhora da Imaculada Conceição, padroeira de Timor-Leste

    This Timorese public holiday on 8 December celebrates the Roman Catholic Feast of the Immaculate Conception. The Virgin Mary under this title is the patroness of Timor-Leste, the world's youngest predominantly Catholic country.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDia de Natal

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    National Heroes' DayDia dos Heróis Nacionais

    National Heroes Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour individuals who have contributed to their nation's independence, freedom, or development. The specific date and honourees vary by country.

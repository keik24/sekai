🇸🇲 San Marino Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in San Marino. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

18 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayCapodanno

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Jan 6
    EpiphanyEpifania

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Thu, Feb 5
    Liberation of the Republic and Feast of Saint AgathaAnniversario della Liberazione della Repubblica e Festa di Sant'Agata

    A public holiday in San Marino on 5 February. It marks the end of the occupation by Cardinal Alberoni's forces in 1740 and honours Saint Agatha, a patron saint of the republic.

  • Wed, Mar 25
    Anniversary of the ArengoAnniversario dell'Arengo

    The Anniversary of the Arengo is a public holiday in San Marino on 25 March. It commemorates the Arengo, the assembly of the heads of families, held on 25 March 1906, which led to the reform of the country's parliament.

  • Wed, Apr 1
    Investiture of the Captains RegentInvestitura Capitani Reggenti

    Investiture of the Captains Regent is a San Marino public holiday marking the semi-annual swearing-in of the two Capitani Reggenti, joint heads of state who serve a six-month term. Ceremonies are held on 1 April and 1 October.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayPasqua

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayLunedì dell'angelo

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Workers' DayFesta dei lavoratori

    Workers' Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May, the International Workers' Day, while some nations observe their own date.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus ChristiCorpus Domini

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Tue, Jul 28
    Anniversary of the Fall of Fascism and Freedom DayAnniversario della Caduta del Fascismo e Festa della Libertà

    The Anniversary of the Fall of Fascism and Freedom Day is a national holiday in San Marino observed on 28 July. It commemorates the fall of the Fascist regime in the republic in 1943 and the restoration of democratic freedoms.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryAssunzione della B.V. Maria

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Thu, Sep 3
    Saint Marinus' Day and Republic Foundation DaySan Marino, Anniversario di Fondazione della Repubblica

    Observed on 3 September, this is the national day of San Marino. It honours Saint Marinus, a 4th-century Christian stonemason from the Dalmatian island of Rab who, by tradition, founded the community on Monte Titano in 301 CE.

  • Thu, Oct 1
    Investiture of the Captains RegentInvestitura Capitani Reggenti

    Investiture of the Captains Regent is a San Marino public holiday marking the semi-annual swearing-in of the two Capitani Reggenti, joint heads of state who serve a six-month term. Ceremonies are held on 1 April and 1 October.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayTutti i Santi

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    Commemoration of the DeadCommemorazione dei defunti

    Commemoration of the Dead (Commemorazione dei Defunti) is a public holiday in San Marino on 2 November, corresponding to All Souls' Day in the Roman Catholic calendar. Families visit cemeteries and pray for the souls of the deceased.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionImmacolata Concezione

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayNatale

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Saint Stephen's DaySanto Stefano

    Saint Stephen's Day is a Christian feast commemorating Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr. It is observed on 26 December in Western Christianity and is a public holiday in several European countries.

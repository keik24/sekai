🇹🇻 Tuvalu Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Tuvalu. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

12 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayTausaga Fou

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Thu, Jan 8
    Te Po o Tefolaha (Golden Jubilee)Te Po o TefolahaRegional

    Te Po o Tefolaha, a holiday on Nanumea, Tuvalu, on 8 January, marking the island's conversion to Christianity; first held in 1922 on the 50th anniversary, it rivals Christmas locally.

  • Tue, Feb 3
    Big Day (Po Lahi, Nanumea)Po LahiRegional

    Po Lahi, a holiday on the Tuvaluan island of Nanumea on 3 February, celebrated with a community feast and marking the day Nanumea embraced Christianity.

  • Wed, Feb 11
    Nukufetau DayTe Aso O TutasiRegional

    An island holiday on Nukufetau atoll in Tuvalu on 11 February, known locally as Te Aso o Tutasi, which honours the island's Tutasi school.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Day of the Flood (Nui)Bogin te IekaRegional

    A local holiday on Nui atoll, Tuvalu, on February 16, remembering the great waves that flooded the island in 1882, which the community survived; it is marked with feasts and dances.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayAso toe tu

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayToe Tu aso gafua

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Wed, Apr 15
    Nanumaga DayAho o te FakavaeRegional

    A local holiday on the island of Nanumaga in Tuvalu, observed on 15 April.

  • Thu, Apr 23
    The Day of the BombingTe Aso o te PaulaRegional

    Te Aso o te Paula, a local holiday on Funafuti, Tuvalu, on 23 April, remembering the Japanese air raid on the atoll in 1943, when it was a US base during the Second World War.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    National Children's DayAso Tamaliki

    National Children's Day celebrates children and their welfare. In Thailand it falls on the second Saturday of January, and in Tuvalu, where it is called Aso Tamaliki, on the first Monday of August.

  • Mon, Aug 3
    National Youth DayAso tupulaga

  • Thu, Sep 17

  • Thu, Oct 1
    Tuvalu DayTutokotasi

    Tuvalu Day is the national day of Tuvalu, marking independence from the United Kingdom on 1 October 1978; it is a public holiday observed on 1 and 2 October each year.

  • Fri, Oct 2
    Tuvalu DayTutokotasi

  • Wed, Oct 21
    Cyclone Day (Funafuti)Aso o te matagiRegional

    A holiday on Funafuti, Tuvalu, on 21 October, remembering Cyclone Bebe, which struck the islands in 1972 and caused deaths and widespread destruction.

  • Wed, Nov 25
    Happy Day (Te Aso Fiafia, Vaitupu)Te Aso FiafiaRegional

    Te Aso Fiafia, a holiday on the Tuvaluan island of Vaitupu on 25 November, marking the final repayment in 1887 of the island's debt to a German trading firm.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayKilisimasi

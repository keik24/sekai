🇲🇴 Macao Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Macao. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

22 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day元旦

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Chinese New Year's Day農曆正月初一

    Chinese New Year's Day, the first day of the lunisolar calendar year, is a public holiday in Macau. It is celebrated with family gatherings, lion dances and fireworks.

  • Wed, Feb 18
    Second Day of Chinese New Year農曆正月初二

    The second day of Chinese New Year is a public holiday in Hong Kong and Macao. Families visit relatives and exchange red packets, and married daughters traditionally visit their parents.

  • Thu, Feb 19
    Third Day of Chinese New Year農曆正月初三

    The third day of Chinese New Year is a public holiday in Hong Kong and Macao. By tradition it is considered an unlucky day for visiting, so many people stay home or visit temples.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday耶穌受難日

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Holy Saturday復活節前日

    Holy Saturday, also called Black Saturday or Easter Eve, is the day between Good Friday and Easter Sunday in the Christian liturgical calendar. It commemorates the time Jesus' body lay in the tomb.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Tomb-Sweeping Day清明節

    The Qingming Festival, or Tomb-Sweeping Day, is a public holiday in Taiwan and Macau on 4 or 5 April. Families honour their ancestors by visiting and tending their graves.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Tomb-Sweeping Day清明節

    Tomb-Sweeping Day, also called Qingming Festival, is a traditional Chinese observance held in early April. Families visit the graves of ancestors to clean tombstones, make offerings, and pay respects.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter Monday復活節星期一

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour Day勞動節

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Sun, May 24
    Buddha's Birthday佛誕節

    Buddha's Birthday is a public holiday in South Korea and Macau on the eighth day of the fourth lunar month. It celebrates the birth of the Buddha with temple visits and lantern decorations.

  • Fri, Jun 19
    Dragon Boat Festival端午節

    The Dragon Boat Festival is a traditional Chinese holiday on the fifth day of the fifth lunar month, commemorating poet Qu Yuan and known for dragon boat races and eating zongzi.

  • Sat, Sep 26
    Day following the Mid-Autumn Festival中秋節翌日

    The Day following the Mid-Autumn Festival is a public holiday in Hong Kong and Macao, falling on the day after the festival on the 15th day of the eighth lunar month.

  • Thu, Oct 1
    National Day of the People's Republic of China中華人民共和國國慶日

    National Day of the People's Republic of China is a public holiday in Macao on 1 October. It marks the founding of the People's Republic of China in 1949.

  • Fri, Oct 2
    Day after National Day中華人民共和國國慶日翌日

    A public holiday in Macao on 2 October, following National Day of the People's Republic of China on 1 October.

  • Sun, Oct 18
    Double Ninth Festival重陽節

    The Double Ninth Festival, or Chung Yeung, falls on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month and is a public holiday in Hong Kong and Macau. Customs include hill climbing, chrysanthemum wine, grave visits and honouring elders.

  • Mon, Oct 19
    Chung Yeung Festival重陽節

    Chung Yeung, the Double Ninth Festival, is a public holiday in Macau on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month. Families visit ancestral graves and honour elders; the day off may move to a weekday when it falls on a Sunday.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' Day追思節

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate Conception聖母無原罪日

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception is a Catholic solemnity celebrating the belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was conceived without original sin. It is observed on 8 December.

  • Sun, Dec 20
    Macao SAR Establishment Day澳門特別行政區成立紀念日

    Macao S.A.R. Establishment Day (20 December) commemorates the 1999 transfer of sovereignty over Macau from Portugal to the People's Republic of China and the founding of the Macao Special Administrative Region.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas Eve聖誕節前夕

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day聖誕節

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

🇦🇴 Angola Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Angola. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

15 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Fri, Jan 2

  • Wed, Feb 4
    Start of the Armed Liberation StruggleDia do Início da Luta Armada de Libertação Nacional

    Start of the Armed Liberation Struggle is a public holiday in Angola on 4 February. It commemorates the beginning in 1961 of the armed struggle for national liberation against Portuguese colonial rule.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Dia do Carnaval (ponte)
  • Tue, Feb 17
    Carnival DayDia do Carnaval

    Carnival Day is a public holiday during each country's carnival season, whose timing varies: in Angola it falls before Lent, in Saint Kitts and Nevis around New Year, and in Sint Maarten in late April.

  • Sun, Mar 8
    International Women's DayDia Internacional da Mulher

    International Women's Day is a global observance held on 8 March celebrating the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women. It is a public holiday in several countries, particularly in the former Soviet Union.

  • Mon, Mar 23
    Southern Africa Liberation DayDia da Libertação da África Austral

    Southern Africa Liberation Day is a public holiday in Angola on 23 March marking the Battle of Cuito Cuanavale (1987-88) and the SADC Liberation Day, honouring the liberation movements of southern Africa.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridaySexta-Feira Santa

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Peace and National Reconciliation DayDia da Paz e Reconciliação Nacional

    Peace and National Reconciliation Day is an Angolan public holiday observed on 4 April. It commemorates the 2002 Luena Memorandum, which ended the Angolan Civil War after 27 years of conflict.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' DayDia Internacional do Trabalhador

    International Worker's Day, also known as Labour Day or May Day, is a celebration of workers and the labour movement held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago.

  • Thu, Sep 17
    National Heroes' DayDia do Fundador da Nação e do Herói Nacional

    National Heroes' Day is a national observance held in several countries to honour individuals recognised for outstanding contributions to their nation. The specific date and honourees vary by country.

  • Fri, Sep 18
    Dia do Fundador da Nação e do Herói Nacional (ponte)
  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' DayDia dos Finados

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Wed, Nov 11
    National Independence DayDia da Independência Nacional

    National Independence Day is a national observance held in many countries to commemorate the date they gained independence from another nation or colonial power. The specific date varies by country.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas and Family DayDia de Natal e da Família

    Christmas and Family Day is a public holiday in Angola on 25 December. It celebrates the birth of Jesus and the value of the family.

🇱🇹 Lithuania Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Lithuania. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

16 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayNaujųjų metų diena

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Feb 16
    Day of Restoration of the StateLietuvos valstybės atkūrimo diena

    Day of Restoration of the State of Lithuania is observed on 16 February, marking the 1918 declaration of independence by the Council of Lithuania. It is one of the two principal national days of Lithuania.

  • Wed, Mar 11
    Independence Restoration DayLietuvos nepriklausomybės atkūrimo diena

    The Day of Restoration of Independence is a public holiday in Lithuania on 11 March. It marks the 1990 declaration by which Lithuania became the first Soviet republic to restore its independence.

  • Sun, Apr 5
    Easter SundayŠv. Velykos

    Easter Sunday, the principal Christian feast, celebrates the resurrection of Jesus. It is the first Sunday after the church full moon on or after 21 March; Orthodox churches reckon it by the Julian calendar, so their date often differs.

  • Mon, Apr 6
    Easter MondayAntroji šv. Velykų diena

    Easter Monday is the Christian holiday on the day after Easter Sunday, following Western or Orthodox Easter by country. It is a public holiday in much of Europe and in many Commonwealth and Latin American countries.

  • Fri, May 1
    International Workers' DayTarptautinė darbo diena

    International Workers' Day, also known as Labour Day, is a celebration of labourers and the working class held on 1 May. It commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sun, May 3
    Mother's DayMotinos diena

    Mother's Day is a celebration honouring mothers, motherhood, and the influence of mothers in society. In most countries it falls on the second Sunday of May, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Sun, Jun 7
    Father's DayTėvo diena

    Father's Day is a celebration honouring fathers and fatherhood. In many countries it falls on the third Sunday of June, but the date varies by national tradition.

  • Wed, Jun 24
    Day of Dew and Saint JohnRasos ir Joninių diena

    The Day of Dew and Saint John (Rasos ir Joninės) is a public holiday in Lithuania on 24 June. It celebrates midsummer with bonfires, wreaths and songs, combining old solstice customs with the feast of Saint John.

  • Mon, Jul 6
    Statehood DayValstybės (Lietuvos karaliaus Mindaugo karūnavimo) ir Tautiškos giesmės diena

    Statehood Day marks a country's founding or restoration and falls on different dates, such as 15 February in Serbia, 30 May in Croatia, 25 June in Slovenia, 6 July in Lithuania, 13 July in Montenegro and 28 September in Czechia.

  • Sat, Aug 15
    Assumption of MaryŽolinė (Švč. Mergelės Marijos ėmimo į dangų diena)

    Assumption Day marks the Christian belief that Mary, mother of Jesus, was taken up into heaven, called the Dormition in Orthodox churches. It falls on 15 August, or on 28 August in churches using the Julian calendar.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' DayVisų Šventųjų diena

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' DayMirusiųjų atminimo (Vėlinių) diena

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas EveKūčių diena

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayŠv. Kalėdų pirma diena

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Sat, Dec 26
    Second Day of ChristmasŠv. Kalėdų antra diena

    The Second Day of Christmas, observed on 26 December, is a public holiday in several European countries including the Czech Republic, Germany, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and the Netherlands, as well as in Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao.

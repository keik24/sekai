🇩🇴 Dominican Republic Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Dominican Republic. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

12 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's DayAño Nuevo

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Mon, Jan 5
    EpiphanyDía de los Santos Reyes

    Epiphany is a Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Jesus to the Gentiles, traditionally represented by the visit of the Magi. Western churches observe it on 6 January; Eastern churches focus on Jesus' baptism.

  • Wed, Jan 21
    Our Lady of AltagraciaDía de la Altagracia

    The Day of Our Lady of Altagracia is a public holiday in the Dominican Republic on 21 January. It honours the Virgin of Altagracia, venerated as the spiritual mother and protector of the Dominican people.

  • Mon, Jan 26
    Juan Pablo Duarte DayDía de Duarte

    Juan Pablo Duarte Day is a public holiday in the Dominican Republic around 26 January, sometimes moved to a nearby Monday. It marks the birth of Juan Pablo Duarte (1813–1876), a founding father of the nation.

  • Fri, Feb 27
    Independence DayDía de Independencia

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good FridayViernes Santo

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Mon, May 4
    Labour DayDía del Trabajo

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Thu, Jun 4
    Corpus Christi

    Corpus Christi is a Roman Catholic feast celebrating the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist; it is a public holiday in several countries.

  • Sun, Aug 16
    Restoration DayDía de la Restauración

    Restoration Day is a holiday in the Dominican Republic on 16 August, marking the 1863 start of the war that restored independence from Spain, and in the Swiss canton of Geneva on 31 December, marking the restoration of the republic in 1813.

  • Thu, Sep 24
    Our Lady of Mercedes DayDía de las Mercedes

    Our Lady of Mercedes Day is a public holiday in the Dominican Republic observed on 24 September. It honours Our Lady of Mercy, the Catholic Marian devotion adopted as patroness of the nation.

  • Mon, Nov 9
    Constitution DayDía de la Constitución

    Constitution Day is a national observance in several countries marking the adoption of their constitution.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas DayDía de Navidad

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

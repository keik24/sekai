🇵🇭 Philippines Public Holidays 2026–2027

Dates, weekdays and local names of public holidays in Philippines. Holidays observed only in some regions are marked.

2026

20 public holidays
  • Thu, Jan 1
    New Year's Day

    New Year's Day marks the start of the year in the Gregorian calendar and falls on 1 January. It is a public holiday in most countries and is widely observed with celebrations, family gatherings, and time off work.

  • Tue, Feb 17
    Chinese New Year

    Chinese New Year, also called the Spring Festival, marks the start of the lunisolar Chinese calendar. It falls between 21 January and 20 February and is the most important traditional holiday in China and across the Chinese diaspora.

  • Fri, Mar 20
    Eid al-FitrEid'l Fitr

    Eid al-Fitr is the Islamic festival marking the end of the Ramadan fast, observed on 1 Shawwal. It is a nationwide public holiday in the Philippines under Republic Act 9177.

  • Thu, Apr 2
    Maundy Thursday

    Maundy Thursday, also called Holy Thursday, is the Christian observance during Holy Week commemorating the Last Supper of Jesus with his disciples. It falls on the Thursday before Easter Sunday.

  • Fri, Apr 3
    Good Friday

    Good Friday commemorates the crucifixion of Jesus and falls on the Friday before Easter Sunday; Orthodox churches follow their own Easter date, which can be weeks later. It is a public holiday in many countries.

  • Sat, Apr 4
    Black Saturday

    Black Saturday is the day before Easter Sunday in the Christian liturgical calendar, commemorating Christ's rest in the tomb after the crucifixion. In the Philippines it is observed within Holy Week as a special non-working day nationwide.

  • Thu, Apr 9
    Araw ng Kagitingan

    Araw ng Kagitingan, the Day of Valour, is a Philippine public holiday observed on 9 April. It commemorates the 1942 fall of Bataan during the Second World War and honours the courage of Filipino and American defenders.

  • Fri, May 1
    Labour DayLabor Day

    Labor Day is a public holiday honouring workers and the labour movement. In most countries it falls on 1 May (International Workers' Day); in the United States and Canada it is observed on the first Monday of September.

  • Wed, May 27
    Eid al-AdhaEid'l Adha

    Eid al-Adha, the Islamic Feast of Sacrifice, is observed on 10 Dhu al-Hijjah. It is a nationwide public holiday in the Philippines under Republic Act 9849.

  • Fri, Jun 12
    Independence Day

    Independence Day is an official public holiday in many countries commemorating the anniversary of their independence from another state or colonial power.

  • Fri, Aug 21
    Ninoy Aquino Day

    Ninoy Aquino Day is a public holiday in the Philippines, usually observed on 21 August and occasionally moved to a nearby day. It commemorates the 1983 assassination of Senator Benigno "Ninoy" Aquino Jr. at Manila International Airport.

  • Mon, Aug 31
    National Heroes' DayNational Heroes Day

    National Heroes Day is a national observance held in many countries to honour individuals who have contributed to their nation's independence, freedom, or development. The specific date and honourees vary by country.

  • Sun, Nov 1
    All Saints' Day

    All Saints' Day, the Christian feast of all saints, falls on 1 November in the West, but in Sweden and Finland on the Saturday from 31 October to 6 November. Most Eastern churches keep it on the first Sunday after Pentecost.

  • Mon, Nov 2
    All Souls' Day

    All Souls' Day is a Christian observance commemorating the faithful departed. It is observed on 2 November by Roman Catholics, the day after All Saints' Day, and includes prayers and visits to the graves of relatives.

  • Mon, Nov 30
    Bonifacio Day

    Bonifacio Day is a national holiday in the Philippines, commemorating Andrés Bonifacio, one of the country's national heroes.

  • Tue, Dec 8
    Immaculate ConceptionFeast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary

    The Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary is a public holiday in the Philippines on 8 December. It celebrates the Catholic belief that the Virgin Mary was conceived free from original sin.

  • Thu, Dec 24
    Christmas Eve

    Christmas Eve is the evening or whole day preceding Christmas Day, observed on 24 December. It is a major cultural and religious occasion in many Christian-majority countries and a public or partial holiday in much of Europe.

  • Fri, Dec 25
    Christmas Day

    Christmas Day is the Christian festival celebrating the birth of Jesus. Most countries observe it on 25 December, while churches following the Julian calendar celebrate it on 7 January. It is a public holiday in most countries.

  • Wed, Dec 30
    Rizal Day

    Rizal Day is a national holiday in the Philippines on 30 December. It commemorates the life and works of José Rizal, the national hero, who was executed on this day in 1896.

  • Thu, Dec 31
    Last Day of the Year

    The Last Day of the Year is observed on 31 December, the final day of the Gregorian calendar year. In many countries it is also called New Year's Eve and is marked with celebrations leading into the new year.

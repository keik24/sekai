Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This policy covers sekai.net, run by KeiK LLC (Japan).

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email address on this site. Settings you change on the news dashboard are stored in your own browser.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies and collects data such as your approximate region, device type and the pages you open. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Some pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors at aboutads.info. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message lets you choose before ad cookies are used, and you can change your choice later from the link in that message.

Third-party content

The news dashboard loads headlines, maps and data from other services. Those services may receive your IP address when your browser loads their content.
